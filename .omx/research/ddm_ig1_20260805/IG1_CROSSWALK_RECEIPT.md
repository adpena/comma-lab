### Seed 2 - Vanishing Condition For Temporal Score Interactions

Verdict: ADOPT as a measurement question for shared context/generator routes.

Honesty: DERIVED. This is not a production claim.

In Pact notation, let a realized packet/solve trajectory have steps `a_t`, state `h_t`, shared parameters
`theta`, and per-step score vector:

`u_t(theta) = grad_theta log q_theta(a_t | h_t)`.

The trajectory Fisher has the form:

`F_traj = E[(sum_t u_t)(sum_t u_t)^T] = sum_t E[u_t u_t^T] + sum_{t != t'} E[u_t u_t'^T]`.

Cross terms vanish only under scoped conditions such as disjoint parameter blocks, receiver/coder separability,
or the same unweighted forward measure with conditional zero-mean score terms. They should not be assumed away
when the exact receiver state, entropy coder, shared table, or terminal acceptance depends on earlier accepted
steps. That is exactly the OD8/TR1 fork shape: independent per-pair solves may be optimal if the packet is
separable; shared context/generator routes are favored if joint remeasure exposes persistent off-diagonal terms.

Named producer: OD8 post-OD3 `solve-persist`/`price-persisted` or the TR1 shared renderer/token line, whichever
first persists the needed native payload values.

Required measurement:

- same persisted stream;
- independent flat/delta/second-order coding versus shared-context/generator coding;
- exact decode equality;
- identical coder family and denominator;
- joint receiver/coder remeasure, not sum of single-step estimates.

Falsifier: exact joint replay shows off-diagonal terms collapse after receiver and entropy coding, or the shared
route loses to independent per-pair coding at equal decoded output.

### Seed 3 - Riemannian-vs-Euclidean Convergence / Exploration

Verdict: ALREADY-EMBODIED plus ADOPT-LOGGING.

Honesty: DERIVED.

Pact already has the main warning: raw Euclidean or raw dual cosines are unsafe without the typed Fisher metric
solve. It also has cap-bound examples where an iteration limit was mistaken for a convergence signal. IG1's
increment is a cheap log format, not a new authority:

- Fisher norm of each proposed/accepted step;
- Euclidean norm of the same step;
- ratio and sign agreement;
- projected marginal S/compute from `trajectory_derived_stopping_law_v1`;
- stop reason (`safety_bound_REPORTED`, `marginal_below_bar`, `continue_projected`, etc.);
- realized receiver/coder acceptance outcome.

Named consumer: `tac.optimization.trajectory_stopping` and fd1/fd2/SQ2/OD3 descendants.

Falsifier: these fields do not distinguish accepted/rejected/cap-bound rows better than existing `stop_reason`,
eta, and realized acceptance logs.

### Seed 4 - GFlowNet-Proper Boundary

Verdict: N-A for the live solver path; TRANSFERABLE only for candidate generation if a future producer needs

Honesty: DERIVED.

The live Pact surfaces are deterministic solve, receiver replay, and byte-closed candidate admission. They do
not currently train a GFlowNet, and they do not need a policy to sample terminal objects proportional to reward.
The GFlowNet framing could become relevant only for a candidate generator whose deliverable is a diverse set of
terminal packet/witness states, such as a future #319 K>1 emission or MC-finisher arm. Even there, it is a
compress-time generator only; it cannot become score authority or a free learned payload.

Named consumer: none now. Possible future consumer: #319 K>1 emission / MC-finisher only after a real charter
requires reward-proportional terminal diversity.

Falsifier for N-A status: a live branch explicitly owns terminal-state diversity and shows deterministic ranking
is insufficient under exact receiver/coder replay.

### Seed 5 - Beyond-Seed Sweep: Graphical Machinery

Verdict: ADOPT as a design lens; no implementation or registry row.

Honesty: INFERRED. Full-text graphical details were not verified in this sandbox.

The plausible Pact mapping is a sparse factor graph whose variables are pair/frame/token/worldsheet streams and
whose factors are receiver support, entropy coupling, shared context, and pose/seg constraints. Separator or
belief-propagation approximations would only be useful if the graph is built from receiver-visible variables and
its messages predict joint replay better than current greedy or Fisher-margin routing.

Named consumer: m91/per-edge graph, worldsheet tracks, and the costate lambda-field, only after a producer emits
a concrete receiver-visible graph.

Falsifier: the factor graph requires hidden scorer/GT state at decode time, cannot preserve exact decode equality,
or does not improve ordering under joint receiver/coder replay.
