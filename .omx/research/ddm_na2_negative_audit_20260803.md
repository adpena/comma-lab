re-anchoring.** Measured here: **5,404** negative-bearing research docs, of which **4,906 (90.8%)

enumeration, and **22 negative-audit artifacts landed in the last 12 days** without draining it.

Three results, in descending confidence:

1. **LAW A — the five charter lenses are ONE defect.** Binary, naive, toy, generic-basis and
   wrong-level are five faces of *the instrument's limit being reported as the object's limit*.
   This is not a framing: `ddm_pu2` **measured** it today. `pu1` read a MODEL WALL off a **6-DOF**
   warp oracle while the shipped `cx1` receiver carries **11 knobs**; direct search on the real
   decoder cut the pair **54.2%** and moved the frontier **ΔS −0.0354283 = 5.414% of the live gap,
   at −3 archive bytes**. pu2 §0.6 states the counterfactual outright: re-pointing the specified
   oracle *"would have measured a 6-DOF floor on an 11-knob vehicle and reported it as a model wall
   — the exact false verdict this arm just overturned."* One operational test falls out (§5.1).

2. **LAW B — I refute the broad reading of my own charter's primary lens.** "Pixel is the wrong
   level" is TRUE for objectives and addresses and **FALSE for actuators**. `ddm_sq1` measured, on
   the same band, same receiver, same 32 pairs: per-pixel **truth-content** paint gives
   `eta_net = −3.7640` (0/32 pairs positive, flips amplified **4.26×**) — the 4th independent NO-GO
   of that family — while per-pixel **SOLVED** paint gives `eta_net = +0.7895` (**32/32** pairs,
   sd 0.0545). Both are per-pixel edits. Granularity is not the discriminating axis;
   **content-vs-solve** is. Banning per-pixel actuation would kill the best realizer we own.

3. **The highest-yield $0 target is an unrun check, not a re-read.** `ddm_na1` (08-02) minted the
   subset-vs-population check and the law that **prefix bias is conservative for an absolute WALL
   verdict but UNCONTROLLED for a COMPARATIVE one** (it is per-archive and does not cancel in a
   difference). That check has **never been applied** to `ddm_rv1`'s 20 re-graded negatives or
   `ddm_a1`'s 34 rows — and those corpora are dense with *comparative* verdicts on six-pair, n16,
   n64, 24/600 and 36-pair subsets. The enabling cure is also still unbuilt: `decode_gt_frame1_pairs`
