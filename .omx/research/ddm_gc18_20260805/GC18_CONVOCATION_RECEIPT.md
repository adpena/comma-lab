## Task-Description Floor Estimate

The task-description floor is bounded by five in-tree facts:

1. **ST2 lower witness is not a floor.** 3,602 B can name a high-recall target prior, but it cannot by itself realize legal RGB/YUV frames or guarantee scorer action.
2. **OD4 is an upper warning, not an upper bound.** 104,775 projected n600 bytes for sparse constraints proves per-flip storage is too expensive, but it prices a bad coordinate system.
3. **PE3 is the live positive hint.** 74,408 B for a generator-pair-bisector/depth-conditioned section covers 83.15% of Road/Lane flips, suggesting the coherent boundary description is measured in tens of KB, not one record per flip.
4. **pp1 direct partition is the explicit control.** 172.2 KB to 173.6 KB for direct partition maps is near the PR130 corridor only with terminal pose and very low d_seg. It is not enough for sub-0.15 unless the learned/task prior absorbs roughly 40 KB to 60 KB.
5. **G4 stationarity is the reason to keep imagining.** 98.806303% static-in-image events argue against per-pair storage for the stable field, but the xi/BEV custody is not closed.
