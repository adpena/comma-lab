# DDM QX1 section census — complete envelope is OVER

**Verdict: `OVER`.** The only complete real-coded envelope available for QX1 as
censused is **153,707 B**, **15,721 B over** the strict `<137,986 B` gate. The
seven-section compact core is **113,844 B**, 24,142 B below the gate, but it is
not a complete object: it omits the required events/exceptions role. The only
retained full-video control for that role is the 39,836-byte S2 event packet. It
stores 17,926 video-derived sites and class transitions, so it is both the
**gate-dominating section** and **inadmissible** under QX1's
generate-not-serialize mechanism.

This does not authorize implementation. The formulation may iterate only by
replacing the explicit-address exception control with an address-free statistic
generated by the implicit partition and then rerunning the complete envelope.

## RECALL EVIDENCE

Searched the full research corpus, canonical equations registry, research
index/DAG, design docs, queue/ledger surfaces, source, and both SSD custody
roots. Queries included `QX1|continuous implicit|two-plane|task-space preimage`,
`selected preimage|irreducible quotient|pose stream`, `event seed|17926|39836`,
`QBW|QBMIX|LC3|pincer`, and `coder race|section`. The canonical registry command
was `.venv/bin/python tools/list_canonical_equations.py --json`; the relevant
laws were the per-stream Brotli boundary, residual-hybrid counted-byte law, and
score/rate marginal laws.

Findings beyond the charter seeds changed the census in four ways:

1. G51's retained direct preimage controls are 400–432 MB. They were retained as
   a closed control, not mistaken for a candidate section; the census prices
   generated planes from compact state.
2. C0B/G49 require strict separation of encoder truth, counted sufficient
   statistic, and generic code. This caused the QBT model to be split by exact
   counted tensor records and prevented target planes or PBR targets from being
   treated as free statics.
3. The v4b 6,705-byte stream is a vehicle-specific warp-parameter field with
   poor pose quality, not a transferable QX1 pose solution. The census instead
   real-coded the retained 600×6 authoritative pose targets and labeled the
   section conditional on joint consumption.
4. The retained S2 task-space event seed is a real 39,836-byte finite packet,
   but it explicitly carries sites and class transitions. This changed the
   planned “empty exception” row into an inadmissible negative control and is
   why the core-only below-gate number cannot clear the formulation.

Queue search found QX1 only in nx1, its arm final message, and the live qx1
queue/spawn rows; no pre-existing QX1 receiver or complete address-free section
packet was found in those searched scopes.

## LIVE-HYPOTHESES

- The 17,926 explicit events may collapse into changes of the continuous latent
  trajectory instead of a separate address stream. This is plausible because
  the core already has 24,142 B of headroom and the current blocker is a
  representation mismatch, not an entropy lower bound.
- A small terminal residual may be feasible if it names generator modes or
  curve parameters rather than sites. This is plausible because G51 closes
  plane serialization, while the retained event packet shows the residual has
  temporal/spatial structure; it has not yet been priced in an address-free
  alphabet.
- The 6,660-byte pose-target stream may be reducible while preserving joint
  conditioning. This is plausible from prior low-rank pose structure, but it
  is secondary: even deleting all pose-target bytes would not make the current
  explicit-address control admissible.
