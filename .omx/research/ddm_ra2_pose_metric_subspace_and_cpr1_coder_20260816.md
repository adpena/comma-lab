## 5. The CPR1 entropy coder — a separate, lossless, and also-closed door

`mp2` raced all 12 Brotli qualities against the shipped `carrier.br` and tied at 0 B. That
races the OUTER coder. The INNER coder — CPR1 itself — had never been raced, and it is
weak by construction: basis under **static order-0 Huffman** over a 5-bit alphabet,
coefficients under **Rice with one k per dimension**. Replacing it is lossless, so `d_pose`
and `d_seg` are exactly unchanged and no scorer needs to run.

Measured, with a REAL adaptive arithmetic coder (not a modelled code length), round-trip
exact on 27,648/27,648 symbols, and a control requiring the shipped encoder to reproduce
the shipped blob byte-for-byte:

| stream | shipped | best adaptive | saving |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| basis (27,648 sym, 3.5523 b/sym) | 12,277 B | 12,046 B | **+231 B** (+32 B dead table) |
| coefficients (7,200 val, 10.9750 b/val) | 9,878 B | 10,293 B | **−415 B — Rice WINS** |

Two named mechanisms, and the second is the more useful one:

- **The basis has no spatial redundancy to harvest.** Contexts on the left neighbour
  (+166 B) and on `|left|+|up|` activity (+231 B) both do WORSE than a context that is
  merely *which of the 12 atoms this is* (+260 B). The atoms differ in amplitude but are
  spatially white — a learned dictionary trained with no spatial prior. Order-0 Huffman
  was already within 0.8% of the adaptive order-0 ideal.
- **The coefficient stream is sample-starved relative to its alphabet.** 7,200 samples
  over 4,096 symbols is **1.76 samples per symbol**. A non-parametric adaptive model
  cannot amortise its own learning cost at that density, so every context I tried made it
  worse (finer contexts monotonically worse: −415 → −486 → −814 → −840 B). Rice's single
  parameter per dimension is the correct model class, not a legacy choice. **This is the
  general lesson for the other sections: alphabet-vs-sample-count, not "arithmetic beats
  Huffman", decides whether a coder swap can pay.**

Net ≈ **+263 B raw**, **+230 B** after Brotli → `ΔS ≈ −1.53e-4`, **1.6%** of the gap. It
clears the −3.5e-6 admission bar by ~44× and is real, but it is not a route to 0.15.

**Honest limit on the 230 B.** I measured Brotli on the *canonical* CPR1 form (22,307 B).
The archive ships a *compacted* body (22,219 B) that `materialize_cpr1` expands into the
canonical one, and I did not reproduce that repack. The raw 263 B is solid; the realised
230 B is **PROVISIONAL** until measured at the repack layer, with the scale of the
uncertainty set by the 117 B difference between my Brotli of canonical (22,278 B) and the

- **The averaged pullback is a relaxation.** The true objective weights each pair by its

  is the standard relaxation. A per-pair-optimal subspace could beat my row — the exact

- **My treatment minimises the PERTURBATION, not the resulting error.** `d_pose` reads
  `‖r_i + J_i δ_i‖²`; I minimise `‖J_i δ_i‖²` and ignore the base residual `r_i`. jc1's
  re-fit did use `r_i` and blew up 1,065× because the linear model was used as a designer.
  Combining the two — subspace projection *plus* a trust-regioned per-pair re-fit — is
  unmeasured and is the live successor.
- **r=11 and r=8 are MEASURED exactly; r=4 is DERIVED** from the measured monotonicity.
- **`d_seg` is not re-measured here**; taken as invariant from four prior treatments. Any
  carrier edit touching frame_1 voids that.
- **`BYTES_PER_DROPPED_DIM = 22,161/12 = 1,846.75`** is assumed uniform across dimensions;
