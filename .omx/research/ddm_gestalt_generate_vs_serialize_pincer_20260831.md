Perfecting the stream that owns a class means every position of that true class becomes correct.

| k | best perfect set | residual | total | vs bar |

**Two of four, not four.** That reads like good news until you price the tolerance:

```
HG1's mycar+movable error alone   113,191  =  88.0% of the ENTIRE mismatch allowance
slack left for horizon+lane        15,433
horizon+lane currently          1,211,842  ->  required improvement  78.5x
as error over their own 73.34% of the field   1.4007%  ->  required  0.0178%
```

**The two streams that are NOT the problem consume 88% of the budget**, leaving 0.0178% error over
three-quarters of the field for the two that are. "Perfect horizon+lane" is not *get good* — it is
*essentially exact description of the Road/Undrivable/Lane partition*.

## 3. WHY THIS IS A CLASS BAR, NOT A FIT BAR

gf1 §3 measured HG1's capacity gap as **target-INDEPENDENT**: 1,325,581 mismatches against GT vs
1,325,033 against lb1's own field — **548 apart out of 117,964,800 (0.04%)**. Its 1.4% is therefore a
property of the generator **class**, not of which field it was aimed at, and not of fit quality.

Composed with §1: a successor cannot be a *better fit* of the same analytic form. It must be a
different class whose bulk-curve error is ~0.018% — two orders below where this class sits — while
its packet stays near 47,603 B. And §6 of the decomposition already showed the free knobs are spent:
the composite order is optimal (all 24 swept), a per-frame horizon shift buys 6.1%, and Movable's
oracle-precision ceiling (34.64% of the gap, the largest single lever) still lands at 3.523×.

## 4. WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CLOSE

**Does not close:** gf1's frontier `packet_B + 0.2909 × mismatches < 85,020` remains open and the
criterion remains one `count_nonzero`. No theorem is in hand. qbw2 said it plainly of its own
negative — *"not a proof that every possible boundary representation is impossible"* — and that
holds here too. Both scopes stand unescalated.

**Does change:** a successor must now clear a **two-sided** bar that no prior memo stated jointly —
better than HG1's class capacity **and** cheaper than qbw2's explicit floor. Previously a candidate
could look alive by beating one side while silently failing the other.
