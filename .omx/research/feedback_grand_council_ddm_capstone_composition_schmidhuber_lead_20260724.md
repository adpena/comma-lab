## The four questions

### Q1 — Is family (d) the true minimal-program family?

**No proof; yes as the first search family.**

The MDL/Kolmogorov reading supports family (d) because it searches descriptions
of the scorer quotient rather than regenerations of the visible scene. That
aligns with all four measured facts:

1. the 159x loss occurs after a valid solved uint8 object enters the
   description-to-RGB regeneration path;
2. the exact raw L3 residual is 497,662,180 bytes and therefore the wrong
   direct coordinate system;
3. the 25-row semantic demand is only 1,569 jointly coded bytes while its
   realization remains open;
4. the 83,838-byte compact-generator row proves only that a small counted
   function can beat direct-plane storage by orders of magnitude; its
   `d_pose=63.030915737` and different objective prevent candidate promotion.

These facts make `(d) > (b) > (a)` a rational acquisition order. They do not
prove that the current DC1 separable fixture, or any one parametric
architecture, is the shortest program. The computable decision is to search
family (d) first while retaining (b) as oracle supervision and (a) as the
typed comparator. #366 is a method inside that search, not a representation
family.

#### Two-part-code boundary

The boundary is content origin, not filename:

- **FREE generic program in `inflate.py`:** a fixed video-independent typed
  interpreter, deterministic real-coder decoders, generic raster/preimage
  algorithms, packet parser, and receiver plumbing. It must work for arbitrary
  legal packets of the declared grammar.
- **COUNTED in `archive.zip`:** named-base bytes, parameters, temporal
  latents, the 25 video-derived placements/choices, exceptions, learned
  dictionaries or codebooks, and every architecture/basis/topology/branch
  selector whose value was chosen after inspecting this video.
- **FORBIDDEN:** scorer weights, ground-truth argmax tables, or moving
  video-derived state into source-code spelling. A content-selected program
  branch is charged even when the implementation of every branch is generic.

This is the only boundary that preserves the legal two-part code without
turning “free code” into an uncounted payload channel.

### Q2 — Is the fit engine the right search?

**Yes after reseal. Add compression progress as a second acquisition
coordinate, never as a new acceptance objective.**

At an accepted state \(q_k\), a candidate \(q'\) is admitted only by the exact
receiver-closed contest action:

\[
\Delta S_{\rm improve}=S(q_k)-S(q')>0.
\]

Let \(L(q)\) be the actual emitted counted bytes of the best deterministic
packet known for \(q\), and let \(w\) be measured candidate work. The event
engine records:

\[
g_S={S(q_k)-S(q')\over w},\qquad
g_L={L(q_k)-L(q')\over w}.
\]

Costate rank proposes coordinates; exact \(S\) admits or rolls them back.
Historical proposal acquisition keeps the nondominated set in
`(g_S, g_L)` and uses the stable typed coordinate ID as the final tie-break.
No coefficient combines the two axes. Thus compression progress can expose a
coordinate group whose representation is becoming simpler, but cannot admit a
candidate that worsens exact \(S\).

This **changes the content, not the count, of SCHED1's ten-item build delta**:

- item 2, the DDM compile target, must type the two-axis acquisition policy;
- item 6, causal event marks, must persist `S_before`, `S_after`,
  `counted_bytes_before`, `counted_bytes_after`, measured work, `g_S`, `g_L`,
  and rollback reason;
- item 9, the semantic/typed hash, must bind that policy and its deterministic
  tie-break.

The other seven items remain exactly as SCHED1 states them. Beta2, EMA,
profile, recovery cap, and wall-clock cap remain `NULL` until their named
derivations exist. No borrowed constant enters the engine.

### Q3 — Does the 25-row residue mean missing program structure or surprisal?

**Undecided. Existing evidence favors “there is structure to test,” not
“structure has won.”**

The independent-to-joint reduction from 4,124 to 1,569 bytes proves shared
description redundancy inside the semantic records. It does not identify a
temporal CONNECTION, and it does not price RGB realization. DM1 contains zero
eligible same-bucket consecutive-pair comparators, so CONNECTION NULL is
custody absence, not family absence.

The discriminating $0 measurement is:

`DDM-CONNECTION-1 exact conditional-codelength probe`

1. From the already SHA-bound solved object, materialize same-bucket
   consecutive-pair supports for every represented bucket that has such a
   pair; do not open RG4 or invent a reachability vocabulary.
2. Encode the later solved record under:
   - a static typed real coder; and
   - a deterministic history program plus its residual.
3. Charge the history-program selector/state and every residual byte, require
   exact parse-back, and evaluate leave-one-pair-out rather than fitting and
   pricing the same row.
4. Report
   `delta_B_connection = B_static - (B_history_program + B_residual)`.

Positive held-out `delta_B_connection` after all state is charged is evidence
that missing generic program structure absorbs part of the residue. No saving
at this formulation is an instance-scoped surprisal result, not a family kill.
dm2 supplies the orthogonal semantic-to-realization ratio and is consumed only
after its receipt and MAIN review.
