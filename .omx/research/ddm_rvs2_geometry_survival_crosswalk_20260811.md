## Answers to the four questions

### 1. Which metric binds camera-lattice compensation?

Use a **current-object composite pullback**, not a single global metric and not raw parameter L2.
For proposal coordinates `theta` at one hard camera state, the local steering object is of the form

```text
G_theta = J_R,theta^T [w_seg G_seg,Fisher + w_pose J_pose^T J_pose] J_R,theta + damping
```

where `G_seg,Fisher = diag(p) - p p^T` is restricted to the frozen five-class rank-4 quotient,
`J_pose` maps the same receiver coordinates into PoseNet's first six outputs, and the local score
marginals determine `w_seg` and `w_pose`. This is a proposal metric, not an exact-score equation:
Seg argmax remains discontinuous, the camera uint8 map is piecewise constant, and archive bytes are
not priced by Fisher geometry. Therefore each accepted hard state refreshes the Jacobians and adds
realized plus/minus lattice secants; exact archive bytes and the recomputed full score arbitrate.

The Bregman and Fisher descriptions are the finite and infinitesimal views of the same categorical
head geometry. The Laguerre/power-cell identity explains the argmax partition, while the Fisher
Hessian supplies natural local scaling. The dual-coordinate law forbids the no-solve shortcut: a
Fisher-natural cotangent requires the supported inverse action, whereas raw dual Euclidean distance
corresponds to a squared-Hessian primal metric. The measured Euclidean/Fisher sign flips require
both signed cosines plus relative norm in telemetry. Euclidean remains a useful optimizer-motion
control, never the decision geometry by itself.

Receipts:

- `optimal_metric_unification_v1`, `bregman_dual_metric_squared_hessian_v1`, and
  `categorical_fisher_trust_region_winner_rival_v1` encode the metric distinction and winner-rival
  trust law.
- The frozen Fisher-curvature/margin receipt reports Pearson `0.978` and 96.8% of flip mass within
  two pixels `[macOS-CPU advisory, n96]`; it supports margin as a local Fisher surrogate, not a
  terminal score.
- MS4D is genuinely `BUNDLE-COMPLETE`: 1,200 Seg buckets, 25 occupied direct rank-4 blocks, Pose
  n600 quadratic custody, composite-R Hessian/adjoint, and matched dual-metric diagnostics. But all
  25 occupied blocks are `UNREACHABLE_BY_COUNTED_COORDINATES` in
  `POST_R_PENULTIMATE_HEAD_QUOTIENT`; the bundle has no current HR1 proposal-coordinate join and
  materialized zero waterfill buckets. It is a schema and prior, not a reusable actuator metric.
- J11 independently proved that a complete scorer-coordinate metric without the proposal's inner
  receiver Jacobian cannot define a null projector.

**Verdict:** Fisher/Bregman proposal geometry enters the reseal at `receiver_bound` as a custody
gate and binds every iteration of Amendment-5's `camera_lattice_compensation` event. Status is
`PARTIAL`: the general metric apparatus is encoded, but the current terminal object's receiver
coordinate pullback and realized secants are missing.

### 2. Should every Seg-touching proposal be pose-null-projected by default?

**No blanket projection. Require a pose-null sibling by default when it is lawfully constructible,
then let the hard complete row choose.** This preserves the exact-zero-pose opportunity without
silently deleting Seg reach or pretending a float kernel survives camera rounding.

The charter seed overstates j11. On its four sealed one-dimensional rays, both learned null
projectors had rank one and nullity zero, so every projected Seg and Pose component was exactly
zero. The exact negative score came from the source-preserving PC1 joint carrier after the split
degenerates, not from a nonzero pose-null Seg component. That is an `INSTANCE` negative for four
one-dimensional rays, not a family negative for higher-dimensional current-object proposals.

The algebraic Q3 fact is nevertheless real at the scorer input: frame-1 YUV6-null AC directions
reach SegNet while costing PoseNet exactly zero in the linear preprocessor. But three receipts bound
its use:

- `pose_null_subspace_is_ac_only_v1` proves constant/DC paint is fully pose-visible; legal
  pose-null edits must be within-cell AC.
- Q31's n32 constrained solve reached only 0.23035 target survival versus a 0.69643 break-even bar,
  with all 32 rows cap-bound and camera clipping causing a 1.04239 mean Pose ratio
  `[macOS-CPU advisory]`. This is a formulation-scoped failed first route, not convergence or a Q3
  family kill.
- ET2's stratified n32 projected-static A/B folded that formulation: Euclidean Q3 eta was 0.04034,
  while diagonal Seg-metric Q3 eta was 0.04396 but failed the pose threshold. Solve-inside-Q3 and
  higher-dimensional receiver-closed projectors remain open.

RVS1's QA92/ST2 receipts still make the positive case for a sibling: unprojected localized paint can
pay large Pose and ERF collateral. The correct policy is therefore projection-first **as a paired
proposal generator**, not projection-only as an optimizer law. The pose-null branch must carry a
SHA-bound `J_pose`, the proposal foreign key, source identity, integer-native realization, receiver
parse-back, and measured Seg-retention ratio. If any are absent, the branch is `NULL`, not zero.

**Verdict:** add a named `pose_null_sibling` sub-event to Arms B-D at proposal formation and again
inside camera-lattice compensation. Prefer it only when it retains the current proposal's useful Seg
span and wins complete hard score. Mandatory projection of every proposal is `FOLDED` at
`FORMULATION(blanket post-hoc/default-only projection)` scope.

### 3. Does xi transport cut exact-call cost in the compensation queue?

**Plausible, but unmeasured on this queue.** The only legitimate current claim is that xi transport
can provide a cheap warm start for the subset that lies inside a retained local trust region.

`#891` derived and implemented

```text
delta(a,b) = -H_ab^-1 [d^2 L / d(a,b) d beta] delta_beta
```

with a positive-definite refusal and one additional beta finite difference. Its n600 scorer-free
receiver census falsified the proposed clip-fraction retirement rule: the full stale set was 267,
zero pairs had an inert partner, and the clamp channel explained only 0.0015% of uint8 changes. About
144 beta-stale pairs were inside the fitted hull and eligible for linear transport; 100 stale hull
escapes required a real joint solve, while 178 pose-drifted pairs had an unanswered trust-region
boundary. The charter's “about 185 pairs” is therefore not an executable denominator; 185 was part
of the beta-axis zero-clamp census, not a proof that those pairs can be retired or transported.

UF1 later found **zero data-complete stale rows** with retained `H_ab` and mixed partials, so it
executed no transport. None of #601/#605, #574, QA39, or the Chasles dual-use law supplies the
missing compensation-solution receipt. In fact, two adjacent rate formulations are scoped
negatives: #574's planar-3 xi predictor added 8,508 B on the already ground-canonicalized Lane
description at n600, and QA39's carried-xi token contexts added 12,262 B or 165,871 B on TR1.
Those close rate/context formulations, not transport of optimizer solutions.

**Verdict:** xi transport enters the reseal as a `PARTIAL` conditional rung after the first exact
accepted pair solution, never as an up-front promise of fewer calls. Race transported warm start
against the existing joint/cold start under the same exact-call budget. Falsify it if transported
starts do not reduce exact realized calls to the same or better accepted complete score, if the
local model residual exceeds its registered radius, or if hull/trust/refusal rates erase the saved
calls.

### 4. Are group-chart optimizers raceable for Arms B-D FiLM/head adaptation?

**Not in the initial race.** Their current evidence supports one late, conditional optimizer fork,

- #469's exact polar-chart design correctly distinguishes raw weight-space Muon from a Stiefel
  tangent treatment. The later MCSD/SPEL implementation is wired and resumable, reconstructs its

  but still `+15.13%`. No real trainer, through-R, current Arm-B/C/D, or n600 verdict exists.
- #552's SPD normal-coordinate momentum is a default-off specification. It explicitly rejects a
  dense 4x4 SPD factor as a replacement for the cheaper exact categorical-Fisher head inverse.
- #556's `W=QH` product chart needs independent Q/H pullbacks, metric IDs, momenta, retractions,
  full-rank-domain custody, exact reconstruction, and split-resume state. Corpus recall found its
  old V9-to-TR1 owner was superseded; the current HR1 routing ledger keeps it deferred until a
  current object, code path, and objective-equivalence receipt exist.

For Arm B, a product chart may eventually apply only to full-rank FiLM/head matrices with an exact
current-object chart. For Arm C, the low-rank adapter factors have their own gauge and must not be
silently treated as an SPD positive factor. For Arm D, hard token coordinates remain categorical or
lattice variables; only its renderer sub-block can inherit a FiLM chart. The analytic rank-4 head
natural gradient remains the control in all cases.

**Verdict:** current-vehicle product-chart optimization is `MISSING` and hypothesis-grade. Queue it
behind the initial AdamW/incumbent arms and fire only after an optimizer-limited wall is measured at a
common checkpoint. Generic MuonH on raw unnormalized matrices and dense-SPD replacement of the
rank-4 head are `FOLDED` at their stated formulations.

## LIVE-HYPOTHESES

- A current-coordinate Fisher/Bregman pullback will reduce wasted compensation calls because the
  exact head geometry is anisotropic and Euclidean/Fisher directions have already changed sign on
  retained trajectories; hard lattice secants should keep the local model honest after rounding.
- A paired pose-null sibling will sometimes dominate the unprojected proposal because QA92/ST2 show
  Pose collateral can erase local Seg recovery, while Q3 proves that nontrivial exact pose kernels
  exist in higher-dimensional frame-1 space. Keeping both branches avoids paying the Seg-reach loss
  seen under blanket projection.
- Xi transport may amortize runs of nearby in-hull compensation solutions because the implicit
  function update uses a retained small Hessian and one mixed-partial finite difference instead of a
  cold joint solve. Its value is most plausible after the first exact pair solution, not before.
- Solve-inside-null using a current Fisher metric may recover more Seg reach than projecting a
  finished field because ET2's algebra preserves the kernel while optimizing the relevant local
  currency; its first diagonal approximation was not enough and does not settle the richer form.
- A Q/H product-chart optimizer may help only if the initial Arms B-D stall for optimizer reasons,
  because the chart matches FiLM matrix geometry and can separate orientation from positive scale.
  The current evidence supports this as a late contingency, not as an initial bet.
