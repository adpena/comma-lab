## 3. THE MECHANISM — a cross-object constant transfer, in one line

The 08-30 §2 derivation held **pose at lb1's 0.00798** while replacing the object that *produces the
frames PoseNet scores*. `#1222` names it exactly: PoseNet scores the FRAMES, so the RENDERER carries
pose — not the stored pose carrier. rb1 swaps the renderer AND the body; inheriting the incumbent's
pose across that swap is `[[m143]]` (cross-regime constant transfer), the same genus that produced
QS4's +2.4e-4 disaster from a stale Schur compensation.

The seg half of that memo is unaffected and stands: the wd3 student targets `original_argmax` (GT)
with the teacher entering only as KL/pose duals, so rb1 genuinely is a correction channel and
genuinely survives bz2d's 99.68× and bo2's 209×. It dies on the axis nobody priced.
