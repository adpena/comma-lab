## LIVE-HYPOTHESES

- A clean HPAC run that actually traverses the discrete-QAT phase will beat the banked 1,870 B estimated joint reduction, because the first QAT step in the earlier run produced its sharpest descent and 28 planned QAT epochs never ran.
- A PR130 model-layout race will recover hundreds of additional lossless bytes, because two independent old objects paid for split/order/cross-tensor transforms and PR130's 73 KB model bundle has not had those transforms raced tensor-by-tensor.
- A controlled model-size versus token-entropy ladder can deliver the largest lossless gain, because A1+A2 owns most bytes and only two uncontrolled operating points exist; the derivative is genuinely unmeasured.
- Narrow free decoder contexts may still pay after PR130's existing contexts, because global position, decoded-boundary state, expert mixing, and motion-conditioned context are absent from the current probability model. The plausible gain is not g4's 18.17%; only a same-object race can price it.
- B-axis byte shaving may be cheap near PR130's very low d_seg operating point, because exact saliency/waterfill tools exist but have never been adapted to the actual semantic tensors and score response.
