## 8. FALSE FRIENDS ruled out (convergent — do NOT build these)

- **BACON/MFN band-limiting** — caps the top frequency = the OPPOSITE of what a thin dash ridge needs (great for smooth boundaries, wrong for the tail).
- **Step-native/heaviside activation "to avoid Gibbs"** — the softmax-of-SDF is ALREADY step-native (discontinuity from argmax of smooth SDFs); no label-field Gibbs. HOSC→square-wave only "parity" with SIREN + unstable.
- **nvdiffrast** — NVIDIA non-commercial + CUDA-only = poison for a portable CPU inflate.py (train-time GPU tool at most; diffvg/hand-rolled simpler).
- **Gumbel-softmax / REINFORCE for the argmax** — unnecessary; clean uint8-STE→SegNet gradient exists.
- **Randomized smoothing / Lipschitz certification** — R is a KNOWN deterministic channel, not a worst-case adversary; norm-ball over-constrains, wastes rate.
- **ETF/ArcFace on the WITNESS head AS the d_seg lever** — indirect (d_seg is SegNet's argmax); at most a prior, only if witness-margin↔SegNet-margin Pearson is high.
- **PSNR-optimal soft-Voronoi (Soft Anisotropic Diagrams 2604.21984)** — same head primitive but WRONG objective (PSNR); confirms our head isn't novel, the task-space-through-R objective is where originality lives.
- **Ship a public CNN prior (supercombo/comma10k-baseline) as a free inflate.py prior** — weights are learned artifacts = COUNTED (tens of MB); only a distilled-tiny prior survives = just the witness again.
- **Compose in logit space ("softmax-of-SDF" literally)** — SegNet reads RGB; α-composite RGB.
- **Assume US 12.19m dash period** — fit (P,δ,φ₀) from the GT longitudinal profile ($0).
