**`verdict_scope: FORMULATION → FAMILY on this object.`**

CLOSED by measurement: **MOVING any of the twelve shipped basis atoms toward a learned direction
fitted to the pose-residual (or pose-weighted target-field) subspace, at fixed rank 12, fixed 5-bit
precision, fixed 24×32 band-limit and fixed int12 coefficient lattice, then re-solving every affected
pair with the canonical solver.** The falsifier fires at every rung from a 12.5 % nudge of the single

statement is now: **on this carrier, every basis change measured to date — precision, rank,
