### Two corrections from the full-version sweep

First, “C3 improved QAT” is not timeless. Cool-Chic v5 measures its decreasing
Gaussian-noise/soft-round proxy as 2.7% better than the original C3 schedule on
CLIC20. The DDM lesson is therefore `ADOPT-RACE`, not “copy C3.”

Second, Cool-Chic v5 strengthens the context lesson beyond v3.4: its IFCE uses
all already-decoded grids and may add hyperlatents used only for entropy
decoding. That is exactly why every DDM stream must race:

`raw ↔ counted tiny ARM/IFCE ↔ g4 free context ↔ CM/CTW ↔ Bellard optional`.

The context model's parameters, hyperlatents, switch header, and sequential
runtime are never free by assertion.

## 3. Four scorer-derived synergies

### RATE-INSIDE-DESCENT

Use the source lesson but preserve Pact authority:

`S_tilde(z) = 100 dseg_tilde(z) + sqrt(10 dpose_tilde(z)) + lambda_B B_tilde_stream(z)`

with `lambda_B=25/37,545,489`. `B_tilde_stream` ranks proposals only. Admission
is exact same-object packed bytes plus receiver/R/uint8/frozen-scorer replay.
EV1 accounting homes are not substituted for a stream.

Status: `ROUTED-PENDING-J8F`.

### QUANTIZATION-GATE CURE

Race three arms with identical coordinates, proposal count, seed, and byte
accounting:

1. exact post-int8/Q8 control;
2. C3 soft-round + Kumaraswamy continuation;
3. Cool-Chic-v5 soft-round + decreasing Gaussian continuation.

All finish hard-forward and roll back on exact realized joint S. #532's
`Delta=62.74` and minimal-writes-die-at-uint8 result mean a proxy win has zero
admission authority. DR1's post-int8 win remains INSTANCE-scoped; it does not
kill other pre-uint8 supports.

Status: `ROUTED-PENDING-J8F-STEP4`.

### COUNTED-TINY-ARM versus FREE-CONTEXT

For every signed, located, receiver-owned typed stream:

`DeltaS_ctx = lambda_B (B_stream,c + B_model,c - B_stream,raw)`

with bit-identical decoded semantics. G4 context is zero payload only when it is
wholly decoder-derived. Cool-Chic IFCE/ARM parameters and any hyperlatent are
counted. Bellard stays optional because its online compute can dominate tiny
streams.

Status: `ROUTED-PENDING-R3-STREAM`.

### BUDGET-SPLIT

Allocate typed DDM sections, pose, engine coordinates, context model, and
headers under one exact budget:

`min Sum_i D_i(b_i), subject to Sum_i (b_i+h_i) <= B`.

Same-pool alternatives use the lower convex envelope; gains telescope along one
archive chain. Cool-Chic's low-rate model-overhead result and v4's joint
frame-rate redistribution justify the split. C3 supplement MAC shares are not
bit shares, so “C3 motion/temporal share” remains `GAP`.

Status: `ROUTED-PENDING-R3-ACTUATOR-EDGES`.
