## E. The new result — the pose screen is linear, so the mp2 closure is scale-conditional

base d_pose 1.4747e-4, not the CUDA 6.886e-6.

**(i) The miss factor is a function of byte credit, not of the edit.** Holding the *measured*
worst-case weight-edit pose damage fixed at mp2 q3/q4's +5.84e-4 (advisory):

| bytes saved | advisory budget | miss factor |
|---|---|---|
| 823 (what mp2 actually tested) | 4.209e-06 | **138.8×** |
| 14,413 (the gap) | 7.371e-05 | **7.9×** |
| 66,117 (the full token drop pool) | 3.381e-04 | **1.7×** |

tested** and becomes 1.7× at the largest pool on the vehicle. verdict_scope on the mp2 family
closure should read **FAMILY(post-hoc weight edits at sub-KB byte credit)**, not FAMILY
unqualified. Consistent with this: the two large-credit levers we hold (rows 5 and 12) were
refused on **seg**, never on this screen.

**(ii) The advisory axis screens ~4.6× stricter than the CUDA axis that actually ships.** The
permissible pose *ratio* at a gap-sized 14,413 B credit is **3.31× base on CUDA** but only
**1.50× base on advisory** — because the advisory base d_pose is 21× higher (#1054), the sqrt is
flatter there, and the ratio bar tightens. Any candidate refused on advisory *ratio* grounds
within ~4.6× of its bar deserves a CUDA re-read before it is called dead. mp2's candidates are
at 139–3,191×, so they stay refused under any transfer model — but the screen itself should
carry its axis label, and the ns1 constructive product (`the $0 pre-proof gate for any future
