# ddm_gb1 admission rule, before candidate pricing

2026-09-09. `[no-triality] [p0-ledger-ok]`. Research only; no score claim.

Scope: four named families, K in {6,8,12}, coefficient-step multipliers
{1,1/2,1/8} relative to each family's explicitly defined scale vector.
These multipliers consume pc2's measured search-reach warning. No solver launches
before a valid complete pricing table. No scorer slot is claimed by this arm.

For a candidate saving b archive bytes, define

    T(b) = min(1.694e-5, (sqrt(10*d_base) + 25*b/37545489)^2/10).

The charter's margin gate is L + 2*P <= T(b), with b >= 2000,
where L is a certified n600 lower bound and P is an absolute d_pose penalty
measured on a matched baseline and lattice. Missing P is UNKNOWN, never zero.
Ratios on another basis or damaged renderer are not P. A lower bound below T
only leaves a candidate possible; it does not prove achievable distortion.
Any later build remains subject to realized n600 measurement and exact bytes.

Source review has already found that pc2's reported Jacobians are six-pair
aggregates and up2's derivative uses straight-through rounding. Thus a
round/project/polish value will be labeled a surrogate candidate value, never
a certified lower bound. For a real map with a certified uniform remainder
epsilon_i, an affine lattice-distance certificate ell_i permits the lower bound
sum_i max(0,ell_i-epsilon_i)^2/3600. Neither ell_i nor epsilon_i is assumed.

Video-fitted right singular vectors are counted, including the stored incumbent
atoms if they are used to turn coefficient-space vectors into spatial atoms.
A fixed seed cannot reconstruct fitted vectors without the fitted information.
A truly generic seeded family may transmit its seed and selected parameters;
it must be regenerated without the video, scorers, targets, or retained Jacobians.

If coverage, remainder, or pricing inputs are missing, every affected table row
is BLOCKED, not REFUSED. The families remain open. The trivial nonnegative
distortion bound zero does not satisfy the charter's lattice-aware pricing gate.
