# ADVISORY — legal SDF-native frame-0 pose inverse carrier — 2026-07-10

`research_only=true`

Authority: mathematical and receiver-design advisory. No training, dispatch, run mutation, score
promotion, or implementation is authorized by this memo.

## Answer first

The current `xi`-only fixed-render pose carrier is too low-rank to support a frontier break by itself,
but the unconstrained frame-0 inverse result proves that the evaluator admits extremely accurate
witnesses. The missing middle is a compact, legal, decoder-native generator: PoseNet may be used
offline to discover and distill frame-0 corrections, but the archive must decode those corrections
without PoseNet, scorer weights, scorer Jacobians, per-pair raster tables, or ground-truth output
tables.

The recommended carrier is

\[
I_{t,0}=W_{\xi_t}(I_{t,1})+
P_\psi\!\left(F_{SDF}(\phi_t,I_{t,1}),\xi_t,c_t\right),
\]

where:

- `I_{t,1}` is the frame-1 SDF witness;
- `W_xi` is the present deterministic motion/warp control;
- `F_SDF` exposes compact geometry features, not a frame raster table;
- `P_psi` is one small shared generator whose video-derived parameters are counted once;
- `c_t` is a small per-pair code whose exact serialized bytes are counted;
- all deterministic bases/construction laws are regenerated by the receiver;
- `P_psi` and `c_t` are admitted only by exact decoded pose gain per final archive byte.

This keeps the original SDF task space at the center. HiNeRV/NeRV is a terminal representation
control, not the pose architecture.

The sharper formulation is a frame0-only Morse-Smale pose fiber in the quotient of the exact Pose
preprocess—not an assumption that six physical motion scalars are sufficient merely because the
evaluator has six outputs.

## 1. The evaluator asymmetry creates a separate frame-0 channel

For each pair, SegNet reads only frame 1, while PoseNet reads both frames after a bilinear camera-to-
384x512 resize and a six-channel YUV construction. Consequently:

- frame1 must satisfy geometry, texture, and pose obligations;
- frame0 has no Seg obligation and should be optimized solely as a counted Pose witness;
- rendering the same partition/texture symmetrically into frame0 spends representational capacity on
  a nonexistent constraint;
- a frame0 Seg-null constraint is unnecessary; the only restrictions are legality, Pose effect,
  archive bytes, deterministic decode, cardinality, and runtime.

The camera resize samples 786,432 of 1,017,336 camera pixels per frame. The remaining 230,904 pixels
are exact resize-blind coordinates, but this is not automatically useful: a blind pixel cannot move
Pose, and code spent there has zero evaluator value.

## 2. Settled evidence and the inference it permits

### 2.1 What is established

- A free optimized frame0 image can drive the official Pose output error to approximately `2.7e-7`
  on the measured probe. This establishes witness existence, not compressibility.
- A 96x128 per-pair image store scales to a rate contribution near `8.56` at n600. That formulation
  is unusable and may not be hidden in code.
- The fixed-render A2/A2+ output-space warp family floors around `1.2-1.5` in its measured objective,
  far from the needed pose regime. It lacks appearance degrees of freedom.
- R1 realized `d_pose=0.001610` at 89,772 bytes on its advisory axis. Near 90 kB and
  `d_seg=0.001`, a candidate must reach `d_pose<9.72e-5` to beat the current local CPU design
  waterline.
- Kalman innovation coding improved pose-code bytes only about 1.5% and made its tested lane about
  14% worse. It kills that small temporal-coder formulation, not the pose-carrier family.
- A recovered local Pose Jacobian summary attributes approximately 54.37% sensitivity to frame0,
  45.63% to frame1, 95.97% to luma, and 4.03% to chroma. The raw receipt for that summary must be
  recovered or remeasured before it is used as promotion evidence.

### 2.2 The required correction to the prior symposium

The prior proposal to recompute a PoseNet Jacobian basis inside `inflate.py` is incompatible with the
current receiver contract. Evaluator models and scorer weights are not legal archive dependencies.
The corrected split is:

- encoder/research side: PoseNet, Jacobians, inverse solves, sensitivity maps, and teacher targets are
  allowed;
- decoder/archive side: only the compact distilled generator/basis, pair codes, deterministic math,
  and legal runtime dependencies are allowed.

An encoder may know the scorer. A receiver may not carry the scorer.

## 3. Local mathematical geometry of the pose channel

Let `p_t(I0,I1)` denote the six Pose outputs for pair `t`, and let a decoded perturbation be
`delta I=(delta I0,delta I1)`. Locally,

\[
p_t(I+\delta I)\approx p_t(I)+J_t\delta I.
\]

The minimum-energy unconstrained correction for a target residual `r_t=p_t^*-p_t(I)` is

\[
\delta I_t^*=J_t^T(J_tJ_t^T+\rho I)^{-1}r_t.
\]

This formula is an encoder-side teacher only. It identifies six output-effective directions inside a
large pixel space. It does not imply six stored image tensors, and it does not belong in the receiver.

### 3.1 Pose-preprocess atoms

Within a 2x2 resize footprint, RGB perturbations can be decomposed into:

- a luma mode;
- two chroma modes per sample subject to YUV mixing;
- three zero-sum spatial Haar modes;
- constant/low-frequency modes that survive bilinear averaging.

The exact six-atom construction in the evaluator advisory describes a local nullspace useful for
frame1 texture. For frame0, the complement is more important: the pose carrier should preferentially
span the luma/low-frequency modes to which the scorer is sensitive. A basis that is visually rich but
projects mostly into the Pose nullspace has poor value per byte.

### 3.2 Rate-distortion metric for distillation

Pixel-space PCA is not the right factorization. Given teacher corrections `Delta_t`, learn a shared
basis `B` and codes `c_t` under the local evaluator metric:

\[
\min_{B,\{c_t\}}
\sum_t \left\|J_t(\Delta_t-Bc_t)\right\|_2^2
+\lambda_B\ell(Q(B))
+\lambda_c\sum_t\ell(Q(c_t))
+\lambda_{dec}C_{runtime}.
\]

For a nonlinear generator replace `Bc_t` by
`P_psi(F_SDF(phi_t,I1),xi_t,c_t)`. The exact outer gate remains the decoded score; the Jacobian
metric only ranks proposals locally.

### 3.3 Global and pair-local MDL split

The generator should be selected by the two-part code

\[
L_{total}=L(\psi)+L(\{c_t\}\mid\psi)+L(headers,decoder,ZIP).
\]

A large shared basis can be optimal if amortized over 600 pairs. A tiny per-pair improvement can be
bad if it adds a symbol/context/header to every pair. The final ZIP delta, not raw tensor bytes, is the
authority.

## 4. Candidate carrier ladder

Every rung is a complete decoder formulation. A higher rung must be compared against the complete
lower-rung archive, not grafted onto a frozen baseline and credited with an impossible counterfactual.

### P0 — `xi`-only control

\[
I_0=W_{\xi_t}(I_1).
\]

Purpose: establish the legal fixed-render floor with the repaired receiver. Expected role: control,
not final carrier.

### P1 — analytic decoder-native residual basis

\[
I_0=W_{\xi_t}(I_1)+\sum_{k=1}^{K}c_{t,k}B_k(x,y,\phi_t,\nabla\phi_t).
\]

Candidate fixed bases:

- low-frequency DCT or cosine packets;
- separable Haar/polyphase atoms aligned to the evaluator resize footprints;
- low-order camera-coordinate polynomials;
- SDF normal/tangent bands around class boundaries;
- radial/affine fields conditioned on `xi_t`;
- junction-centered atoms for motion-critical geometry.

Only the small `c_t` table is video-derived if `B_k` is fully deterministic. This is the cheapest
legal disambiguator.

### P2 — scorer-metric learned global basis

Learn `B` offline from teacher corrections using weighted low-rank factorization, then serialize and
count `B` once. Decoder evaluation remains a matrix/basis expansion with no scorer.

Advantages: exact bounded runtime, transparent bytes, stable quantization. Risk: a globally linear
basis may require too many modes because corrections are conditional on scene geometry.

### P3 — SDF-conditioned coordinate generator

Use a small coordinate network with inputs such as:

\[
(x,y,t,\phi_c,\nabla\phi_c,\kappa_c,margins,junction\ features,\xi_t,c_t).
\]

Possible portable functions include sinusoidal, Gaussian/WIRE-like, or band-limited BACON-like
features, but the receiver must have a deterministic NumPy reference and measured MLX/Torch parity.
The relevant comparison is final archive score, not paper benchmark PSNR.

### P4 — low-rank hypernetwork/modulation

A small shared decoder emits a limited subset of generator coefficients from `c_t` and SDF summary
statistics. Restrict it to low-rank/diagonal modulation to control bytes and runtime. This rung is
admissible only if P2/P3 show strong conditional residual structure.

### P5 — sparse hard-pair sidecar

After a global carrier, sort residual proposals by exact score value per byte. Admit only a sparse
set of hard-pair corrections. The sidecar must have explicit indices, entropy model, decoder section,
parse-back tests, and final-ZIP accounting.

## 4.1 Preferred quotient-space decoder

Let `phi_1` be the decoded frame1 class potentials and define compact task-native features

\[
\chi(\phi_1)=
[\text{soft class weights},\ \text{signed distances/margins},\ n,\ \kappa,
\ \text{fixed polyphase/wavelet coordinates},\ \xi].
\]

Rather than generate arbitrary camera RGB directly, generate the six local Pose-preprocess
coordinates per 2x2 scorer block,

\[
q_0=(Y_{00},Y_{10},Y_{01},Y_{11},U,V),
\]

through

\[
q_0=\Pi_{feasible}
\left[q_{base}(\phi_1,\xi)+B_\omega(\chi(\phi_1))z_t\right].
\]

Then apply a deterministic inverse-YUV map and a bounded integer camera-grid preimage/lift `L(q_0)`.
Pixels outside the exact sampled resize support receive a generic deterministic fill. This makes the
receiver optimize in the quotient actually seen by PoseNet, while retaining a legal camera-resolution
raw output.

Away from clamps, one explicit inverse is

\[
R_j=Y_j+1.402(V-128),\qquad
B_j=Y_j+1.772(U-128),
\]

\[
G_j=Y_j-0.344136(U-128)-0.714136(V-128).
\]

The feasible projection must account for RGB range, uint8 rounding, clipping, and the bounded
integer preimage. The quotient grid has 192x256 blocks because each block represents four luma
samples on the 384x512 scorer grid.

- `omega` is counted once if learned/video-derived;
- `z_t` is counted per pair;
- analytic modes, generic fill, and lift code are regenerated and uncounted as data;
- frame1 bytes/raw/hash stay frozen during a frame0 carrier experiment, so Seg is structurally
  identical;
- no scorer, teacher output, Jacobian, or raster target ships.

For residual

\[
r_t=P(Q(L(q_{0,t})),I_{t,1})_{1:6}-y_t
\]

and local code Jacobian `A_t=partial r_t/partial z_t` evaluated at the current decoded/quantized
state, a proposal step is

\[
\Delta z_t=-A_t^T(A_tA_t^T+\mu I)^{-1}r_t.
\]

This Levenberg-Marquardt step is encoder-side only and must be relinearized after every accepted
quantized update. Select candidate analytic/SDF modes by pivoted QR or a D-optimal criterion

\[
\log\det(A_tA_t^T+\epsilon I)-\lambda\,L_{exact}(mode),
\]

with shared modes admitted before sparse hard-pair exceptions. The high-dimensional mode library
aligns the local six-output fiber; a fixed six-DOF physical warp need not span that fiber.

## 4.2 Proposed counted grammar

Use a distinct typed pose-fiber section rather than silently overloading the current `XIP2`/SE(3)
semantics. The section should contain:

- version, pair count, quotient grid/channels, basis kind/dimension, quantizer, coder, and lift-law
  hash;
- optional existing `xi_eff` control stream;
- globally shared fitted basis/generator weights;
- quantized per-pair pose codes;
- sparse hard-pair indices and innovations;
- exact scales and entropy-model state.

Do not store homographies derivable from `xi`, evaluator weights, Pose targets, full/coarse per-pair
rasters, or values for unsampled camera pixels. Fixed analytic modes and the lift contain no learned
payload, but their receiver implementation still contributes to final archive bytes and runtime.

## 4.3 Matched complete-artifact arms

Freeze one frame1 SDF artifact and require identical frame1 raw SHA-256 for every frame0-only arm.

| Arm | Complete receiver formulation |
|---|---|
| C0 | `xi`-only control on the same frozen SDF artifact |
| C1 | SDF-conditioned luma/polyphase pose fiber |
| C2 | SDF-conditioned full YUV6 pose fiber |
| C3 | C2 plus sparse hard-pair innovations |
| C4 | shared-trunk co-adaptation only if C2/C3 fail, with exact d_seg rollback guard |

basis. Compare lower envelopes at actual ZIP bytes; never equalize with padding or dead sections.

## 5. Raw coefficient waterlines

The following are raw coefficient payloads before entropy coding, headers, alignment, and ZIP effects.
They are sizing heuristics, not archive claims.

| K coefficients/pair | quantizer | raw bytes at 600 pairs | rate-score cost |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6 | int8 | 3,600 | 0.002397 |
| 6 | 12 bit | 5,400 | 0.003596 |
| 8 | int8 | 4,800 | 0.003196 |
| 12 | int8 | 7,200 | 0.004794 |
| 12 | 12 bit | 10,800 | 0.007191 |
| 18 | 12 bit | 16,200 | 0.010787 |

Shared generator costs:

| final archive delta | rate-score cost |
|---:|---:|
| 5 kB | 0.003329 |
| 10 kB | 0.006659 |
| 20 kB | 0.013317 |
| 30 kB | 0.019976 |

At 90 kB, the sub-0.15 target is infeasible at `d_seg=0.0010`, requires approximately
`d_pose<4.029e-5` at `d_seg=0.0007`, and permits approximately `d_pose<1.606e-4` at
`d_seg=0.0005`. R1's `0.001610` pose debt is about 40 times the favorable `d_seg=0.0007`
waterline. This is why the pose fiber and the SDF partition must improve together.

At pose debt `d`, the first-order score gain from reducing pose by `epsilon` is approximately

\[
\Delta S_{pose}\approx -\frac{\sqrt{10}}{2\sqrt d}\,\epsilon.
\]

Because this marginal grows as `d` shrinks, pose does not become irrelevant near the frontier. The
exact nonlinear difference must be used for acceptance:

\[
\Delta S_{pose}=\sqrt{10(d+\Delta d)}-\sqrt{10d}.
\]

For a control/candidate pair at fixed Seg and base bytes, the largest additional byte budget paid for
by a pose improvement is

\[
B_{max}=\frac{\sqrt{10D_{control}}-\sqrt{10D_{candidate}}}{\lambda_B}.
\]

At R1's illustrative `D=0.001610`, halving pose debt pays for about 55,814 additional bytes, a
tenfold reduction pays for about 130,299 bytes, and perfect pose pays for about 190,560 bytes. Adding
7,195 new bytes would require `D<0.001490717` to pay for itself. R1 already contains its 7,195-byte
pose section, so this last number is a marginal illustration, not a re-accounting of R1.

## 6. Offline teacher-to-decoder pipeline

### Stage A — freeze custody

Pin source video, evaluator, resize/raw operator, source checkpoint/archive, seed, runtime, hardware
axis, and pair order. Build a content-addressed target store on the SSD tier. No durable evidence may
live only in `/tmp`.

### Stage B — construct inverse targets

For each pair:

1. decode the exact current frame1 witness;
2. initialize frame0 from the legal `xi` warp;
3. solve an encoder-side full-image or minimum-norm pose correction;
4. project through the exact uint8/resize/raw chain during optimization;
5. preserve target hashes, output residuals, convergence, and failure status.

These target images are research artifacts, never candidate payload.

### Stage C — analyze intrinsic dimension

- singular spectrum in pixel L2 and evaluator metric;
- conditional spectrum after removing `xi` warp;
- coefficient entropy by pair/time segment;
- clustering by road geometry, motion, and residual direction;
- easy/hard pair distribution;
- sensitivity to quantization and fresh decode.

### Stage D — fit the smallest legal carrier

Fit P1, then P2, then P3 only if the previous rung cannot cross the waterline. Keep basis/generator
size and pair-code dimension independent. Quantize during fitting or use a deterministic
quantization-aware finisher.

### Stage E — compile and parse back

- serialize all video-derived generator parameters and pair codes;
- regenerate analytic banks from versioned constants;
- reject unknown/unconsumed tensors;
- inflate twice in fresh processes;
- assert raw identity and exact cardinality;
- evaluate the exact component score.

### Stage F — exact selection

For each atomic mutation, record:

\[
v_j=-\frac{\Delta S_j}{\Delta B_j}
\]

with component deltas and interactions. Select the complete archive at the global waterline, not the
lowest proxy loss checkpoint.

## 7. SDF features that make the carrier task-native

The pose generator should consume a compact derivative of the original level-set state rather than
relearn an RGB video codec:

- signed distances `phi_c` and top-two margins;
- normalized gradients/normals and tangents;
- curvature and boundary scale;
- class area and centroid summaries;
- island/birth/persistence identifiers;
- junction type and local edge incidence;
- temporal screw/advection residual;
- motion code `xi_t`;
- frame1 texture summary only where it moves Pose.

These features give the generator scene geometry at low incremental cost because they are already
available from `G`. Receiver computation is free only in score bytes, not in contest time; runtime
must still be measured.

## 8. Essential disambiguators

### D1 — fixed basis versus learned basis

Match final archive bytes and pair-code dimension. Compare exact d_pose, generator runtime, and hard-
pair tail. A learned basis wins only after its counted global weights.

### D2 — unconditional versus SDF-conditioned generator

Hold parameter/byte budget fixed. If conditioning does not reduce exact residual or code entropy, it
is complexity without task value.

### D3 — linear versus nonlinear generator

Match bytes, not parameters. Compare quantized fresh-decode outputs. Nonlinearity is justified only
by a favorable complete-artifact delta.

### D4 — shared coefficients versus sparse hard-pair sidecar

Compare uniform K across 600 pairs with a smaller global K plus indexed corrections for the tail.
Include index/context/header bytes.

### D5 — frame0-only versus joint frame0/frame1 pose finishing

Frame1 has a Seg constraint; frame0 does not. Admit joint correction only if its frame1 pose benefit
exceeds both its bytes and any Seg regression after interaction.

### D6 — local Jacobian metric versus exact nonlinear teacher

The local metric is cheaper but can fail across uint8 boundaries or network nonlinearities. Compare
target ranking and final archive ranking; retain the Jacobian only if rank agreement is adequate.

## 10. Failure taxonomy

- `FORMULATION-REFUSED`: a fixed basis, generator size, or quantizer misses the waterline.
- `RECEIVER-REFUSED`: serialized state is ignored, unknown, or nondeterministic.
- `LEGALITY-REFUSED`: scorer weights, raster tables, hidden data, or unauthorized dependencies appear.
- `RATE-REFUSED`: exact archive cost exceeds exact pose gain.
- `RUNTIME-REFUSED`: decode violates the budget.
- `PAIR-TAIL-REFUSED`: mean improves but hard pairs dominate the nonlinear pose term.
- `PARADIGM-REFUSED`: only after the legal analytic, learned-linear, conditioned nonlinear, and sparse-
  tail families all fail under measured favorable budgets.

A failure of `xi`-only or one basis is not a failure of the SDF witness paradigm.

Hard falsifiers for the carrier experiment:

- any frame0-only arm changes the frozen frame1 raw hash;
- a nonzero pose-section mutation leaves frame0 raw unchanged;
- an accepted surrogate/Jacobian update worsens exact decoded `sqrt(10*d_pose)+lambda_B*B`;
- the encoder-side free-frame reachability canary fails under the same scorer/input custody;
- C1/C2 cannot beat C0's complete-artifact lower envelope;
- SDF conditioning matches coordinate-only conditioning at equal receiver bytes;
- the active code Jacobian has rank below six and the design adds more physical twist instead of a
  missing output-effective mode or sparse exception;
- a temporal predictor fails to save final ZIP bytes;
- decode requires PoseNet, targets, or per-pair rasters.

## 12. Exact blockers

1. The current canonical receiver does not yet consume all proposed v7.5.3/v8 state.
2. The current `xi`-only complete-artifact floor is not remeasured under that repaired receiver.
3. The old full inverse targets are not packaged as a complete, content-addressed n600 teacher set.
4. The recovered Jacobian sensitivity summary lacks its raw receipt.
5. No legal shared generator/basis plus pair codes has a final-ZIP byte receipt.
6. No carrier has demonstrated the required `d_pose` waterline at a plausible `d_seg/B` operating
   point.
7. Pairwise interactions with frame1 geometry/texture remain unmeasured.
8. Decode runtime, portable parity, and deterministic raw identity are unproven for P2-P4.
