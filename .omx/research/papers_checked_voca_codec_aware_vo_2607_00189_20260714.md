## What it says (MEASURED from the body, verbatim-grounded)
Causal **stereo** visual odometry that exploits **video-codec information** to improve tracking on lossy-compressed streams. The codec contribution is narrow + precise:
- **Only H.264 motion vectors + I-frame structure** are used (residuals/quantization deliberately NOT).
- **Mechanism = warm-start init for KLT photometric tracking, NOT a pose signal.** §3.3: negate the codec MV → initialize the 2D translation (`−d_k → t_2D`), identity rotation (`R_2D = I`), then optimize the SE(2) transform by photometric error (standard KLT objective, Eq. 7). The codec's ONLY role is the initialization warm-start.
- **Key honest caveat (their own):** "Directly replacing KLT-Tracking with decoded motion vectors is not a viable alternative" because MVs are "optimized for compression rather than motion fidelity." The value is that "motion vectors can bridge displacements far larger than what image pyramids alone can recover" → a cheap LARGE-DISPLACEMENT prior.
- **Dual-track consistency reject (§3.5):** track each point WITH and WITHOUT the MV prior; if both succeed, reject if inconsistent (kills false-positive MVs pointing to visually-similar regions).
- Stereo, causal (real-time), Basalt architecture, no loop closure, handles monochrome/YCbCr.
