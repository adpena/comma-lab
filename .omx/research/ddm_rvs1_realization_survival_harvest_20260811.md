## Result

The harvest found **22 distinct receipted mechanisms/rules**. Six are fully encoded in HR1/HR2/JS1, five are only partially encoded, nine are missing as named treatments, and two are measured exclusions rather than launchable techniques. The charter predicted at least 12 mechanisms and at least three design misses; both thresholds clear.

The highest-value missing unit is **camera-lattice quantize-then-compensate**. PR133/135 show why: direct basis quantization made Pose error about 29 times worse on the tested control, while quantizing and then re-solving the nearby int12 coordinates through the exact scorer produced the competitive row. HR1 Arm D has joint compensation in spirit, but neither HR1 nor JS1 names the camera-uint8 rounding boundary as a distinct compensation event. The successor should hard-round the camera bytes, hold that realized lattice state, and re-solve only the remaining nearby renderer/token/carrier degrees of freedom before accepting the complete score.

The second design miss is a **free receiver micro-race** inside Stage 0: exact incumbent order, PR98/L28 channel correction, camera-pre-R coverage/placement variants, and the already-derived semantic hood treatment must be alternatives on the same terminal bytes, never additive credits. The older L28 result is confounded with a Brotli change and the paint/hood rows are often harmful, so this is a discriminator—not a promised win.

The third miss is a **survival-force ablation** for Arms B-D: exact R-in-loop is already mandatory, but dynamic quantum selection, through-R reachability saliency, component margin floors, QAT staging, and the PR95 sigma schedule are not independently represented. They must be raced one at a time against the R-in-loop baseline; a combined treatment would destroy attribution.

No result here changes the live score. ## Reseal delta

The JS1 reseal should preserve HR1/HR2 as the baseline and add only the following named deltas:

1. **Stage-0 free-treatment fork:** incumbent camera order; L28 only; one token-derived camera-pre-R coverage alternative; one token-derived hood alternative. Select one child by complete V5 score; do not sum their deltas. Fixed stored strokes and the historical post-MENU1 hood reassert remain folded.
2. **Survival-force ablation:** exact R baseline; then independently test QAT staging, dynamic quantum proposals, `S_R×margin` allocation, component margin floor, and sigma noise. Warm Muon is conditional on selecting Muon at all.
3. **Camera-lattice compensation event:** after hard camera rounding, re-solve the remaining legal nearby DOF, relinearize after every accepted hard state, and remeasure the entire remaining queue.
4. **Joint hard admission:** every accepted artifact is a retained complete archive/decoded payload with exact Seg, Pose, and byte accounting. No intermediate/proxy row is banked.

These changes do not create a fifth main learned-prior arm. The sigma treatment is an ablation nested inside the learned arms, and the free receiver treatments are Stage-0 children. The four-arm A-D comparison remains intact.

## LIVE-HYPOTHESES

- Camera-lattice compensation is the likeliest new survival gain because PR133/135 demonstrate that coarse damage can be more than recovered when the remaining coordinates are re-solved through the exact scorer; HR2 provides the exact hard camera boundary needed to test the lifted form.
- L28 may still provide a free terminal improvement because it changes exactly three camera channels at the last legal decode-side point, but its Pact evidence is confounded and only a same-terminal isolated child can settle it.
- `S_R×margin` plus dynamic quantum may improve exact-call efficiency because one selects receiver-reachable fragile sites and the other selects the first amplitude that can actually cross their hard cell boundary.
- Component margin floors may protect thin Lane/Movable births better than the global teacher-margin mean because a single erased component can be hidden by improving many easy pixels.
- PT1's amplitude-statistics result may transfer as a renderer regularizer because SegNet's projection error depends strongly on regional RGB statistics; this is more plausible than another fixed edge stroke after QA92/ST2.
- PR95 sigma noise may add robustness beyond exact R+QAT if it explores nearby learned-state quantization basins, but this remains the weakest positive hypothesis because its isolated contribution was never measured.

## DEAD-ENDS

- Direct coarse quantization without compensation is closed at the tested PR133 formulation: atom-9 Pose error worsened by about 29 times. Never fire CBQ alone.
- Minimal/sub-quantum real-valued writes are closed as a realization strategy: they vanish at uint8. Select a measured whole-lattice rung instead.
- Raw UNIWARD texture weighting is closed for placement on the measured witness surface: its reachability alignment is chance-level and mildly misdirected. Use through-R reachability plus margin.
- Fixed prototype/GT Lane compositing is closed at the QA92/ST2 formulations: target recovery was overwhelmed by neighborhood and Pose collateral. A re-rendered manifold solution is a different formulation.
- Static hood reassert stacked after the MENU1 winner is closed at that n600 instance: all stored and decoder-derived variants worsened the joint objective.
- Treating L28+L32 Z8 as an isolated L28 measurement is closed: the byte and distortion changes were co-applied.
- Treating the PR95 sigma schedule as measured camera noise is closed: the retained recipe supports learned-state noise only, and its isolated effect is unknown.
- One-shot stale linearization and additive composition credits are closed: v17 exposed negative validity ratios and v19b measured interaction. Relinearize and remeasure after every accepted hard state.
- Float-first pose-null/range exactness followed by rounding is closed where an integer-native solver exists: #532 and DK1 show the lattice can destroy the float claim.
- Reusing an advisory, prefix, small-n, or scalar-loss result as a terminal population score is closed by the instrument and authority receipts; ordered arrays, exact batch shape, retained payloads, and the complete hard row are required.
