## VERDICT

**`BASIN-CERTIFIED / STRICT-MINIMUM-REFUTED / AFFORDABLE-DISCRIMINATOR-EXISTS`.**

The PR130/PR135/AFR1 body is a local basin on the evidence: every originally registered sharp-optimum
direction lost; no perfected single incumbent axis reaches sub-0.12; the four-body Cross has no measured
rate-and-distortion meeting point; fixed-context coder, section, tolerance, and transport variants are
drained at their measured scopes. That is a real certificate against doing another ordinary same-body
tweak.

It is **not** a certificate of a literal sharp local minimum in every direction. Two later receipts
puncture that reading: FCD1 found a same-object coder-favourable edit direction, and AFC1 physically
landed a zero-damage, zero-stored-context **−81 B** escape on the exact body. The correct claim is:

> AFR1 is locally optimized with respect to the enumerated, measured same-body perturbation classes;
> small receiver-causal context escapes remain, while no measured direction closes the 42,016 B
> fixed-distortion demand.

`ccs1` is a **basin boundary**. It holds the decoded AFR1 field and distortion fixed, so it is not an
exterior vehicle; it changes the receiver-causal probability schedule, so it is not another member of
the closed fixed-context interior. It owns that boundary rung and is live independently of this memo.

The alternative-vehicle audit does **not** harden the basin into “the achievable optimum lives here.”
Across seven vehicle/form scopes, only the retired fixed TR1 endpoint earns an optimal-form closure;
five are under-form and the semantic-primary full-vehicle race was never measured. The cheapest
decisive escape discriminator is therefore the matched semantic-primary full-vehicle race, after the
live `ccs1` boundary returns terminal.
