## Executive verdict

Nielsen's Hilbert simplex geometry (HSG) gives exact and useful mathematical
coordinates for positive probability rays, but this intake produces no new
adoptable Pact lever. H1--H3 are already covered by stronger, receiver-relevant
surfaces. H4--H5 remain open families with named, zero-dollar exit
measurements; neither is authorized for the live builder from this memo.

The primary-source delta is:

\[
d_H(p,q)=\max_i\log\frac{p_i}{q_i}-\min_i\log\frac{p_i}{q_i}
=\|\log p-\log q\|_{\mathrm{var}}.
\]

Thus HSG is projective on positive rays and becomes a polyhedral variation norm
in normalized log coordinates. Its Voronoi cells are polytopal in those
coordinates. This is not, by itself, the frozen SegNet head's exact Euclidean
Laguerre/power diagram, nor is the exact nonsmooth HSG norm Fisher--Rao
geometry. The HSG-LSET smoothing is a distinct differentiable geometry and
must never be relabeled as exact HSG.

### H1 -- projective gauge versus the measured gauge/nullspace

**Verdict:**
`ALREADY-HAVE-BETTER(#519/#580 + segnet_head_affine_gauge_quotient_v1 + #553 PDW2)`.

**MEASURED:** independent positive ray scaling preserved HSG distance to
`3.55e-15`; applying the same positive diagonal projectivity to both points
preserved it to `1.78e-15`. But one-sided positive diagonal scaling changed
100% of sampled distances, and a shared diagonal transform changed 8.0859375%
of fixed argmax labels. HSG therefore supports the already-known ray/common-
shift quotient, not an unrestricted per-coordinate gauge of the frozen scorer.

**Verdict scope:** HSG as an additional explanation or byte lever for the
measured SegNet gauge/nullspace surfaces. #519 found no packet win from the
fixed-shaped quotient projection; #553 PDW2 and the nullspace program already
expose more receiver-relevant structure.

### H2 -- HSG polytopes/tropical coordinates versus exact power cells

**Verdict:**
`ALREADY-HAVE-BETTER(exact Laguerre/power + #581 comparator + #557 coder races)`.

**DERIVED and MEASURED:** HSG distance on log probabilities equals logit
variation distance; the n64 maximum discrepancy was `3.55e-15`. HSG Voronoi

**Verdict scope:** an HSG/tropical representative as a smaller exact

polytopal algorithms for unrelated consumers.

### H3 -- HSG argmax-boundary distance versus the knee ladder

**Verdict:**
`ALREADY-HAVE-BETTER(frozen_scorer_fisher_curvature_margin_colocation_v1 + segnet_head_rank4_linear_flipdist_v1 + exact uint8 secants)`.

For Nielsen's max-minus-min convention, the HSG distance from a logit point to
its nearest top-1/rival equality boundary is exactly the top-1/top-2 logit
margin. The n64 numerical error was exactly zero. That scalar is already the
Fisher/margin ranker. The exact rank-4 Euclidean feature-space flip distance is
stronger for a realizable edit because it divides each rival margin by its
head-pair normal norm; sampled normal norms ranged from `2.60180622897` to
`4.00736390498`. HSG margin and rank-4 flip distance had Spearman
`0.956860045646`, high but not identical. The exact integer/uint8 secant ladder
then closes the receiver surface that neither continuous distance supplies.

**Verdict scope:** HSG output-cell boundary distance as a replacement for
realization-aware flip cost. The mathematical identity remains useful as a
derivation, but a duplicate canonical equation would create two names for the
existing margin law, so none is appended.

### H5 -- HSG clustering/centers versus strata and movable-site coding

**Verdict:**
`N-A-WHY(relative significance -18 payload-only bytes excludes codebook/table overhead and nonuniform radius; named exit full same-#557 packet with all overhead and n64 exact secants)`.

The primary clustering reference uses farthest-point geodesic-center updates
with step `1/(t+1)` and farthest-first k-center seeding; it does not supply an
entropy code. On real n64 frozen logits with four centers per class, a static
range-coded assignment payload was 517 B for HSG farthest-first and 535 B for
Euclidean farthest-first, and all streams parsed back exactly. The apparent
`-18 B` excludes center/codebook and frequency-table bytes. The per-class HSG to
Euclidean maximum-radius ratios were
`[1.0179692574, 1, 1, 0.9958603954, 1]`, so the distortion result is not
uniformly favorable.

`movable_site_coder` encodes absolute `(cx, cy, w, h)` geometry and tracks sites
with Euclidean/Hungarian costs. Projective normalization would discard absolute
position/scale information required by that grammar. HSG logit strata may be
tested as a distinct class-codebook family, but they are not a drop-in movable-
site coordinate replacement.

**Verdict scope:** the real n64 frozen-logit class-stratum k=4 proxy only. Exit
requires the same inputs and complete #557 packet, with every center, table,
header, and payload byte counted, decoded integer/uint8 n64 hard-oracle secants,
