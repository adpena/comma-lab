# Contest-native codec gestalt and suppressed-signal consumption audit

## Disposition

No frontier score moved in this pass. The effective competitive target is the
minimum selected by `.omx/state/canonical_frontier_pointer.json`; at the time of
this dated audit it is `0.172 [official-leaderboard display]` from PR130. The
local `0.1910828242` original baseline and `0.1880443979880752` banned defensive
bank are not the competitive target.

The project is not short of mathematical or engineering pieces. It is short of
one identity-preserving compiler path that carries a candidate from semantic
coordinate through application, receiver realization, exact joint score,
byte-home ownership, entropy coding, archive parse-back, and the next consumer.
The strongest current evidence says the project has repeatedly mistaken a
missing join between those representations for missing physics or a dead
family.

The resulting system verdict is:

`CODEC_PIECES_REAL_COMPOSITION_LINKER_AND_COUPLED_CONTROL_LAW_MISSING`

This is a PRECONDITION/APPARATUS verdict. It is not a negative on DDM, v7.5/v8,
semantic witnesses, INR backends, pose carriers, residual carriers, or learned
compression.

## The game is a frozen-evaluator codec

The decoded video is not the object being faithfully reconstructed. It is a
legal witness in the quotient induced by the frozen evaluator:

- Seg authority: last-frame RGB after the real uint8/resize receiver, reduced
  to frozen SegNet argmax cells.
- Pose authority: the official two-frame YUV6 PoseNet output.
- Rate authority: exact counted archive bytes.

The codec therefore stores video-specific sufficient statistics and compiles
them through a deterministic generic decoder into any receiver-visible witness
that lands in the same or a better evaluator cell. Human fidelity is useful
only when it causally improves those cells.

The representation should be treated as six cooperating streams, not one
monolithic neural video:

1. a static semantic/worldsheet stream for frame-1 class topology;
2. a frame-0 geometry/motion/pose stream;
3. dynamic object and event tracks;
4. boundary, island, and pair-local innovation streams;
5. realization/quantization state that survives uint8, resize, and parse-back;
6. entropy/container state with exact byte ownership.

HiNeRV, SNeRV, HNeRV, low-rank carriers, analytic rasterizers, pose splines,
DDM programs, and sparse sidecars are interchangeable backend/codeword
families within those streams. None is the system objective by itself.

## The coupled score surface replaces independent component gates

The exact law is

\[
S(d_s,d_p,B)=100d_s+\sqrt{10d_p}+25B/37{,}545{,}489.
\]

The target is a three-dimensional sublevel set, not one independently fixed
value for Seg, Pose, and rate. At an operating point, the differential is

\[
dS=100\,dd_s+\frac{5}{\sqrt{10d_p}}\,dd_p
   +\frac{25}{37{,}545{,}489}\,dB.
\]

The Pose exchange rate changes with `d_pose`; a fixed global `R*` or a fixed
box cannot represent the surface. Exact finite before/after `delta_S` remains
the admission authority because receiver and argmax effects are discontinuous
and action composition is noncommutative.

The new coupled-surface audit gives concrete derived examples at target 0.172:

- the local `0.1910828242` triple can keep its distortions only at most
  148,510 bytes; no Pose-only repair can work because its Seg plus rate terms
  already exceed the target;
- PR130's rounded displayed components imply a 190,839-byte conditional rate
  boundary, while the published 191,052-byte row reconstructs to
  `0.17214129749`; therefore rounded public components cannot be used as exact
  archive authority;
- `(d_seg=0.0002, d_pose=0.00002331, B=200000)` is inside at
  `0.168439...` even though its bytes exceed historical 130 KB thinking;
- `(d_seg=0.0007, d_pose=0.00002331, B=130101)` is inside at
  `0.171896...` despite its larger Seg term;
- a measured/derived transition with worse Seg and enough rate improvement is
  correctly admitted, while a Seg improvement with excessive byte expansion is
  correctly rejected.

The old `d_seg<=0.00116`, `d_pose<=0.00161`, `B<=130101` corner scores about
`0.3295`, proving that three separately plausible-looking caps do not define a
useful target.

## The right-under-our-noses finding

Four substantial composition layers already exist:

1. `tac.action_effect.v1` is a shared exact Seg/Pose/rate currency with
   authority, normalization scope, receiver support, payload sections, hashes,
   parse-back/inflate survival, and exact nonlinear `delta_S`.
2. `tac.action_commutator_ledger.v1` already refuses additive fantasy, compares
   only identity-compatible actions, and queues unmeasured ordered composites.
3. DDM has receiver-closed application mechanisms and exact evidence:
   - MS7's pair-523 PF3 control closes coordinate, application, uint8 effect,
     same-object Seg/Pose effect, L3 byte home, coder owner, parse-back, and a
     129,797-byte Brotli object;
   - J8F maps a DM4 proposal descriptor into integer J5 application, reemits
     exact archive bytes, and measured a range/gauge arm with
     `delta_S=-0.052757084057141856` at unchanged 138,804 bytes;
   - J12 supplies proposal foreign keys, full receiver-coordinate Jacobian
     custody, a source-preserving PC1 adapter, and exact two-base joint pricing.
4. `src/tac/v2_compose/` already implements store/generate/learn separation,
   deterministic bulk generation, residual target and bundle, pose sidecar,
   and a four-section archive grammar.

Yet current DDM producers do not emit `tac.action_effect.v1`, the DDM waterfill
does not consume the cross-family commutator currency, and the current routing
cards do not name `v2_compose`. The compiler existed, the effect currency
existed, and exact actions existed, but their types never met.

The missing macro layer is therefore a linker, not another research ontology:

`semantic coordinate -> counted application -> receiver effect -> ActionEffect
-> measured commutator/waterfill -> v2 archive section -> exact one-SHA replay`

Every linked action must preserve one stable semantic action ID or an explicit,
evidence-backed alias across all stages. It must also carry:

- source coordinate and parent-state SHA;
- application callable/operator and validity radius;
- receiver support and uint8/resize/parse-back survival;
- exact finite Seg, Pose, and rate endpoints through the same receiver object;
- representation type and L1-L5/stream byte home;
- coder owner and counted container range;
- downstream consumer and negative verdict scope.

If any field is absent, the action is retained as typed incomplete signal. It
is never silently dropped, zero-filled, or promoted.

### Fresh apparatus bugs found and extincted in this pass

The linker audit immediately falsified four existing assumptions in executable
code:

1. `ActionEffect.archive_sha256` is the candidate/output archive, but the
   commutator compared the three candidate hashes as though they were a common
   base. Legitimate actions from one base therefore appeared incompatible.
   `base_archive_sha256` and `base_payload_sha256` are now additive v1 fields;
   candidate identity remains separate.
2. Composite lookup accepted action-ID substring conventions. IDs such as `A`
   and `AA` could bind the wrong parents. `composed_action_ids` is now an exact
   ordered tuple, legacy matching is exact-only, and duplicate measured
   composites fail closed.
3. The default commutator "measurement command" merely reran the ledger. It
   applied no codeword and could never generate a composite receipt. The fake
   default is removed; only an explicit real materializer template is accepted.
4. The inverse-action artifact reader ingested both a four-arm wrapper and its
   nested `four_arm_ablation`, minting the same composite twice. Wrapper-aware
   traversal now consumes the evidence once.

The anti-orphan contract now exists as
`tac.applied_action_receipt.v1`. It foreign-keys one codeword and concrete
operator/version to ordered parents, base/candidate archive and payload hashes,
physical edge, support/bucket, integer quantum and validity radius, exact
receiver/R identity, changed-uint8 hash, five-type/L1-L5 stream home, coder
owner, exact ActionEffect, authority, provenance, and verdict scope. It remains
`research_only=true`, `promotion_eligible=false`, and `score_claim=false`.
The next obligation is adapter coverage for J8F, PF3/MS7, J12, and CC3/E5A;
the schema itself does not manufacture those foreign keys.

## Why PF3 is not a family negative

The bounded PF3 materialization found 29/37 occupied buckets, measured 16
single-coordinate edges, and found all 16 worsened joint distortion while
adding 772-939 marginal E4 bytes. It therefore produced 0/162 finite
conditional prices. Fifty-two bounded probes remain unmeasured.

This result has two narrow meanings:

1. those 16 directions are not useful single-coordinate openings at that
   exact parent;
2. the current RD1 schema lacks the application/foreign-key bridge needed to
   make the measurements into cellwise duals.

It does not show that the representation family is dead. J8F and J12 both show
that profitable openings can be joint even when scalar decompositions are
zero or adverse. The next PF3 move is therefore not indefinite enumeration of
new scalar directions. It is a small number of exact, identity-compatible
joint openings selected by existing curvature, ActionEffect, and commutator
evidence, while the remaining 52 probes remain a bounded control arm.

## The rate/distortion ontology mismatch

EV2 established that the current C1 archive's 134,211 bytes are not lawfully
separable into pair-by-cell byte owners. Its coarsest lawful rate partition is
seven stream homes. Distortion is still observed over 162 semantic cells.

Forcing a 162-by-162 diagonal price table on that object creates null duals by
construction. The correct object is a bipartite incidence/Jacobian:

\[
\Delta d_c = \sum_h A_{c,h}(a_h), \qquad
\Delta B = \sum_h \Delta B_h,
\]

where `c` is a distortion cell, `h` is a counted stream home, and an action on
one stream may move many cells. The seven-home waterfill and a rebuilt C1 with
independently coded substreams are both lawful arms. They answer different
questions and must run in parallel; neither should be disguised as the other.

CC3 already supplies one exact seven-home-style rate row: 3,422 bytes removed
with receiver-identical output (`delta_S=-0.0022785693`) on its measured stream
shape. This is a real inherited export primitive, not evidence that every
stream receives the same gain.

## Suppressed-signal and sharp-edge taxonomy

### 1. Component gate suppresses an improving joint move

The optimal-start history records a realized W_seg `x+` move with negative
joint `delta_S` that was rejected for Seg regression. Any raw component cap may
remain only as a catastrophic safety guard and must report whether exact
joint score would have accepted the move.

### 2. Endpoint/state category errors

J7 proved that endpoint metrics plus bytes do not constitute a resumable start
state. A candidate needs archive path/SHA/parse-back plus compatible optimizer
and stage state before a launch consumer may select it.

### 3. Identity/foreign-key loss

PF2 membership, receiver actuator, application coordinate, RD1 cell, and byte
home identities were introduced in different generations. Missing joins were
then interpreted as zero effect or absent mechanism. Every schema migration
must preserve the original semantic action ID and aliases.

### 4. Granularity mismatch

Distortion cells and rate homes need not have the same cardinality. Null
cellwise byte prices on a jointly coded stream are a formulation result, not a
waterfilling negative.

### 5. Single-action thinking in a noncommutative receiver

PF3 single directions were adverse; J8F range projection and J12 PC1
composition were beneficial. Ordered composites must be measured and carried
in the existing commutator ledger, never assumed additive.

### 6. Partial byte accounting

The previous v2 Phase-B report charged the ZIP while omitting an external
counted keyframe payload. The compose tool now charges the honest total. Every
candidate must have a bijection from counted archive bytes to a decoder-
consumed section or an explicit container-overhead home.

### 7. Stale duplicated objective/target constants

`compose_witness_archive.py` carried a hard-coded `0.19110` target and duplicate
score logic. It now reads the effective canonical pointer, uses shared score
geometry, and derives conditional boundaries. No durable executable may carry
a snapshot frontier as control law.

### 8. Numerical warnings treated as survivable noise

J12 observed divide-by-zero, overflow, and invalid-value warnings in the PC1
warp matmul. Finite uint8 output is not enough for promotion-grade confidence.
The receiver must either prove the intermediates irrelevant and sanitize under
an exact equivalence test or fail closed on nonfinite intermediates.

### 9. Negative-scope collapse

J3's four-step regression, P1's shared low-rank linear solve, PF3's 16 scalar
directions, and MS2R's q4/q8 raw-stream control are all formulation/instance
results. None closes nonlinear, pair-conditioned, scorer-solved, joint-action,
or alternative-description children.

### 10. Orphaned compiler and findings surfaces

`v2_compose`, five-type/L1-L5 typing, same-parent DM4 columns, and several
execution-custody lessons existed without current routing consumers. This audit
routes them below.

## Architecture vehicles, all sharing the linker

The correct portfolio is not one arbitrary substrate and not an unbounded idea
list. It is three governed vehicles with common codeword/effect/container
interfaces:

### Vehicle A: live optimal descent

Continue the v7.5/J10/J12 lineage as a residual-trunk optimizer. Admit only
exact joint improvements, preserve per-stage EMA/resume state, and measure
decay/stop laws. This vehicle exploits the current description family.

### Vehicle B: support-renewing semantic codec

Byte-close v8/v9 per-class carriers in increasing complexity: MyCar static
mask control, Lane analytic ground band, Road/Undrivable boundary field,
Movable sparse islands. The purpose is to change reachable support, not polish
an atlas proven unable to close the target.

### Vehicle C: frame-0 Pose carrier

Reactivate P1 only as a nonlinear real-PoseNet descent family. The negative on
the shared low-rank linearized solve remains binding for that formulation.
Frame 0 is Seg-free by evaluator incidence, making this the cleanest hedge
against slow PC1 Pose decay. PR130 is a frozen-space existence proof, not a
source of transferable constants or code.

All three emit the same `ActionEffect` plus applied-action receipt and compile
through one hardened packet surface. `v2_compose` supplies reusable framing,
deterministic inflate, and byte accounting, but does not own the scientific
representation. Their single actions and ordered composites are
ranked by exact expected `delta_S` per wall-clock and bytes, with uncertainty
and information gain kept explicit.

## Autonomous selection policy

At every scheduling point, rank admissible actions using a vector, not one
hand-set component threshold:

- expected exact `-delta_S` and uncertainty;
- wall-clock to first falsifier and to one receiver-closed candidate;
- counted bytes and runtime/inflate cost;
- representation novelty/orthogonality to the current portfolio;
- whether it closes a missing linker edge or only adds another proxy;
- probability of producing reusable system intelligence even on a negative.

The default next action is the highest expected information-adjusted score
movement that is reversible, lane-claimed, storage-safe, resumable, and
contest-faithful. Small work is chosen only when it unlocks or falsifies a
larger vehicle; it is not the default unit of ambition.
