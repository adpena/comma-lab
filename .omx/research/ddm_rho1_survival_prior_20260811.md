## Definition and conversion law

For one same-parent, same-axis terminal comparison,

```text
rho = (d_base - d_cand) / (d_base - d_target)
    = (0.00029639352578669786 - d_cand) / 0.00014443294093669787.
```

`rho = 0` means no C1 semantic gain survives, `rho = 1` means all of it survives, `rho < 0` means the
terminal candidate is worse than cp135, and `rho > 1` means it beats the semantic C1 target. A ratio
from another receipt converts numerically to rho only when its numerator and denominator describe the
same before/target/realized endpoints. Otherwise this memo either shows a clearly labelled
**rho-equivalent diagnostic** or records the conversion as **not identifiable**.

ET1's eta has the same algebraic units:

```text
eta = (flips_before - flips_after) / n_described.
```

Thus `eta = rho` only if `flips_before`, `n_described`, and `flips_after` are respectively the cp135
baseline debt, the C1-removable debt, and the terminal C1-on-cp135 debt. ET1 uses a different vehicle,
actuator, described set, and receiver, so its eta is an analog rather than a converted rho row.

RP1 shows why they are not in tension with cell survival: only `42,816/117,964,800 = 0.0362956%`

`166.5x` separation. A large norm error can remain inside almost every argmax cell because the decision
is set-valued and margin-protected. The admissible lesson is to optimize the integer lattice and fragile
margins, not to treat the A-space maximum as rho.
