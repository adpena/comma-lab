## Node contracts

### A0-A1 — semantic custody

Bind source, evaluator, hardware axis, legal support, observable, and exact equality relation. Use
the foundations PER on ambient `X` and the setoid quotient on its legal support `D`. No algebra node
may silently widen `D` or replace statistic equality with a tangent/proxy relation.

### A2 — constructive action

Take the reflexive/symmetric/transitive closure of admissible receiver-valid arrows. Prove closure,
observable preservation, and `E_H subseteq E_U`. Record fiber-completeness separately; failure to
span the full statistic fiber is a nonnegative constructive quotient debt.

### A3 — extension split audit

Type the wide normal kernel/coefficient bundle, covariance quotient, strict action functor/object
compatibility, section, and overlap maps. A typed literal `K rtimes H` is split and has a neutral
extension class; current notation is not by itself an implementation proof of those data. State
dependence alone cannot emit an `H^2` verdict. For a nontrivial factor system, charge only positive
conditional information in the section-invariant extension class, retain `(alpha,omega)` for a
nonabelian kernel, and account for section/table realization separately.

### A4 — finite orbit accounting

For every finite constructive action, record group order and a reproducible fixed-point-count table.
Burnside yields the exact orbit count. The full score-fiber group gives one orbit per score pair and
an ideal <=64-bit class-label ceiling on the frozen evaluator, but is nonconstructive.

### A5 / R1 — representation and v8

Only decompose a finite induced image or compact/unitary chart. Covariance alone sees the five class
fields as multiplicity copies. Scorer-fixed class idempotents make class-channel bookkeeping
canonical, not allocation. The actual v8 edge payload/index/incidence action remains to be typed, and
rate allocation still needs measured costates/KKT. Reject “FiLM intertwiner” unless its declared
action satisfies the affine equivariance identities and receiver parity.

### A5 / R2 — module structure induction

Solve the intertwiner constraints or Reynolds-project candidate maps `A` into `Hom_H(V_i,V_j)`
before proposing shared bases, tying, VQ-in-loop, or module-aware low rank. Use invariant Gram metrics
and multiplicity-block ranks before SVD. A nonzero intertwiner is only an admissible coupling, not a
correlation/byte verdict. The #461 transpose remains a legal paying exact storage chart because its
inverse runs before semantics; it need not commute with `H_cov`. Route survivors to #110/#242 and
require a fresh exact archive gate.

### G1 — invariant lattice

Use `H -> A^H` and `B -> Stab(B)` as an antitone Galois connection. Walk only closure-fixed nodes.
Global stratified use requires compatible restriction maps and effective descent; otherwise remain
per-stratum.

### A6-A8 — apparatus authority

The receiver must select a representative without prohibited scorer/source cargo, reproduce the
declared transitions, survive parse-back, and win on exact packed bytes. Only then does exact
statistic equality on a named contest axis permit pointer review.
