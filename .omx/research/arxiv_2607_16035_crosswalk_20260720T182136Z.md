## 1. Exact mechanisms

### 1.1 Prevalent embeddings, not state reconstruction

The paper begins from the fractal Whitney embedding prevalence theorem. For a
compact `Theta` of box-counting dimension `p'`, and integer `k > 2p'`, almost
every smooth map

\[
\Phi:\mathbb{R}^{p}\to\mathbb{R}^{k}
\]

is one-to-one on `Theta` and is an immersion on compact smooth-manifold pieces
inside `Theta`. “Almost every” is prevalence in an infinite-dimensional
function space, not merely residual/Baire genericity.

The target differs from Takens-style state reconstruction. The object to be
identified is the finite-dimensional model parameter `theta`, and the
coordinates are expectations of functions of noisy observations. The paper's
central promise is therefore parameter-law separation, not recovery of a
latent trajectory or a stable finite-bit code.

### 1.2 Random cosine features of lag blocks

For a length-`m+1` observation block
`x=(x_1,...,x_{m+1})`, with each `x_j in R^d`, Eq. (1) draws

\[
\varphi_i(x)=\cos\!\left(\sum_{j=1}^{m+1}\Omega_{i,j}\cdot x_j+\alpha_i\right),
\quad
\Omega_{i,j}\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}N(0,I_d),\quad
\alpha_i\overset{\mathrm{iid}}{\sim}U(-\pi,\pi),
\]

and stacks `k=2p+1` such features. The lag order is supposed to match the
model's dependence order, e.g. an order-`m*` Markov process uses `m=m*`.

The feature law is data-independent and bounded. Boundedness is load-bearing
in the smoothness proof because integration against a signed measure obeys a
sup-norm times total-variation bound.

### 1.3 Stationary/asymptotically mean-stationary estimator

For observed feature vectors `f_t^obs` and the mean over `s` simulated
realizations `bar(f)_t^sim(theta)`, Eq. (4) forms time averages

\[
F^{\rm obs}=\frac{1}{n-m}\sum_{t=m+1}^{n}f_t^{\rm obs},\qquad
\bar F^{\rm sim}(\theta)=\frac{1}{n-m}\sum_{t=m+1}^{n}\bar f_t^{\rm sim}(\theta).
\]

Eqs. (5)-(6) estimate `theta` by minimizing the norm of their discrepancy.
Assumption 1 requires uniform weak convergence of the time-averaged lag-block
laws to a limiting `P_theta`. Assumption 2 requires:

1. compact `Theta subset R^p` with nonempty interior;
2. a bijection `theta -> P_theta`;
3. a Frechet-`C^1` extension of that law-valued map in total variation.

The expectation map is

\[
\Phi(\theta)=\int \varphi(x)\,dP_\theta(x). \tag{7}
\]

Theorem 2 proves this map is `C^1` under those assumptions and invokes the
prevalent-embedding result once `k>=2p+1`. If it embeds, the population
discrepancy is nonzero away from the true parameter.

### 1.4 Nonstationary rolling-window estimator

For nonstationary paths represented at rescaled time `u=t/n`, Eqs. (9)-(11)
replace global averages by rolling averages `F_t` with a window `w`. The
criterion uses lagged rolling discrepancy `Delta_{t-L}` and a correction term
coupling it to the current observed-versus-simulated feature residual. The
paper prescribes

\[
L_n=\left\lceil \tfrac{1}{10}\log^2 n\right\rceil,\qquad
w_n\in[n^{1/2},n^{3/4}],\qquad \tau_n=w_n,
\]

with `w_n` chosen by a stated prediction-error criterion.

At each `u`, Assumptions 3-4 permit a Lipschitz reparameterization
`g_u(Theta)=tilde(Theta)_u` and require a locally bijective, Frechet-`C^1`
law family. The local feature map is

\[
\widetilde\Phi_u(\widetilde\theta_u)
=\int\varphi(x)\,d\widetilde P_{\widetilde\theta_u,u}(x). \tag{13}
\]

Because Lipschitz maps do not increase box-counting dimension, Theorem 3 uses
the original `p` to retain the conservative `2p+1` bound. Assumption 5 is the
global distinguishability condition: any two original parameter values must
induce different local laws on a positive-Lebesgue-measure subset of times.
Then

\[
Q^{\rm RW}(\theta)=\int_0^1
\|\Phi_u(\theta_0)-\Phi_u(\theta)\|^2\,du
\]

is positive away from `theta_0`.

### 1.5 Proof mechanism and an assumption boundary

The smoothness proof factors the expectation through the bounded linear map
`T_phi(mu)=int phi dmu`. Frechet differentiability of the law-valued map gives

\[
D\Phi(\theta)[h]=\int \varphi\,d(Dp(\theta)[h]),
\]

and the remainder is bounded by `||phi||_infinity` times the total-variation
remainder. Continuity of `Dp` similarly gives continuity of `D Phi`; Whitney
extension supplies a `C^1` continuation outside the parameter set. The final
step applies the prevalent-embedding theorem.

**REVIEW CAVEAT, theorem-application scope:** The paper establishes that the
expectation maps generated by admissible features are `C^1`, and separately
that embeddings are prevalent among `C^1` maps. Its sentence that the specific
Gaussian random cosine draw “will, almost surely” yield an embedding also
needs the induced random-feature distribution to avoid the shy/non-embedding
set. The v1 statement does not spell out that bridge as a condition on `D` or
prove that this particular pushforward law is a prevalence probe. Therefore
this crosswalk treats `2p+1` as a generic-map sizing principle under the
paper's assumptions, not a universal almost-sure certificate for any chosen
random-feature distribution. This caveat is formulation-level; it does not
reject the Whitney/prevalence theorem or the paper's practical estimator.

### 1.6 Experiments and their evidentiary limit

- **Henon map:** parameters `(a,b,sigma)`, so `p=3` and `k=7`; only the first
  state coordinate is observed with Gaussian noise. Figure 2 plots parameter
  estimate densities for `n=100` and `n=1000` around
  `(1.4,0.3,0.1)`.
- **Lorenz-63:** parameters `(sigma,rho,beta,lambda_1,lambda_2,lambda_3)`, so
  `p=6` and `k=13`; all three state coordinates are observed with Gaussian
  noise. Figure 3 plots estimate densities for `n=100` and `n=1000` around
  `(10,100.5,8/3,1,1,1)` over horizon `T=1`.

These figures illustrate concentration with larger `n`. The paper does not
provide numeric error tables, coverage, repeated-seed counts, optimizer
details, runtime, ablations versus fewer/more features, or finite-sample
conditioning diagnostics in the experiment section. They are useful
illustrations, not transferable empirical constants or a stability guarantee.

## 3. Bounded mathematical checks

### 3.1 Paper's Gaussian illustration

For Figure 1(a), the paper uses
`X(mu) ~ N((mu,mu^2,mu^3), I_3)` with `p=1`, hence three features. The Gaussian
characteristic function gives the exact expectation

\[
E[\cos(\Omega_i^T X+\alpha_i)]
=e^{-\|\Omega_i\|^2/2}
  \cos\!\left(\Omega_i^T(\mu,\mu^2,\mu^3)+\alpha_i\right).
\]

A fixed-seed NumPy check (`seed=260716035`) evaluated this analytic map and
its derivative on 4,001 points over `mu in [-2,0]`:

## 4. Honest regime caveats

1. **Identification is not stable estimation.** One-to-one and immersive maps
   need not have a well-conditioned inverse. The paper gives no lower bound on
   the smallest singular value of `D Phi`, no finite-sample error for the
   examples in this paper, and no quantized inversion bound.
2. **Generic is not task-optimal.** A generic feature may separate parameters
   while spending all its sensitivity in scorer-null directions. Pact values
   features by realized score debt per exact byte in the Fisher/margin metric.
3. **Distributional moments are not receiver programs.** Expectation features
   live at the estimation boundary. Any video-derived values stored in
   `archive.zip` are counted, and decode still owes deterministic RGB bytes
   within 1,800 seconds and exact parse-back through `R`.
4. **The assumptions are substantive.** Bijectivity of `theta -> P_theta`,
   Frechet-`C^1` total-variation dependence, compactness, correct lag order,
   simulability, and positive-measure path distinguishability must be checked;
   they cannot be inferred from a good optimizer fit.
5. **The costate organ is data-starved.** One real trajectory does not become
   multiple independent regimes by drawing many synthetic simulations. The
   paper supports a simulator-identifiability diagnostic; it does not relax the
   real chronological walk-forward gate.
6. **The experiments do not transfer.** Their sample sizes, parameter recovery,
   Gaussian observation models, and visual concentration have no relation to
   `100*d_seg + sqrt(10*d_pose) + 25*bytes/37,545,489`.
7. **No contest evidence was produced.** No archive, scorer, VJP extension,
   receiver, or exact CPU/CUDA path ran in this arm.
