## 2. Three places where the charter's own framing did not survive the memos

The charter handed me five laws with numbers attached. Three of the attachments were wrong, and saying so
is more useful than a clean table.

1. **hpr1's "secant" does not belong to the refit.** The charter said "ΔB = Δmodel + Δtail; the secant reported".
   The decomposition is exactly right — `+351` model and `−1,238` tail give `−887 B`, and that re-derives
   independently from the archive totals `180,246 → 179,359`. But **no secant is reported for the retrain, and the
   arm explicitly refuses to attach one**: "a refit, not a capacity rung". The only secant hpr1 reports, `−1.2066`,
   belongs to the shape rung R1 — which was **falsified** (+812 B against this very control). Registering a secant
   here would import an instrument the producer rejected, so the equation carries none, and says why.
2. **obx2's ceiling is not 4.0e-4.** The closure memo states `4.0e-4`, but the **burn spec's pre-registered hard
   gate** is tighter: `d_seg < 3.1990253844713356e-4` at pointer-like `d_pose = 4.59e-6` (and `2.8765207719095085e-4`
   at `d_pose ≤ 1e-5`) — "hard gates, not predictions". `4.0e-4` is a rounded restatement **25 % looser** than what
   was pre-registered. The equation defaults to the pre-registered gate and keeps `4.0e-4` only as a named
   constant. On this object the choice changes nothing (5.77× over the loose ceiling, **7.22×** over the real one),
   but on a successor it decides the burn.
3. **sr5 never says "placeholder hash".** Its words are the literal `sha256: "SKIPPED_LARGE_STREAMED"` and then
   **"That is not a hash."** The deeper form the arm actually states is *a certificate that has never been redeemed
   is a promise, not a property*, with its sister *a gate that has never refused anything is not known to be a gate*.
   The anti-pattern is registered under the arm's own framing, not the charter's paraphrase.

A fourth, smaller one: pc3's under-pricing **ratios** (1.389× cheapest per-dimension, 1.131× global ÷2) are
**DERIVED** by this arm from pc3's paired price tables — pc3 states the prices, never a ratio — and are labelled
DERIVED in the anchor.
