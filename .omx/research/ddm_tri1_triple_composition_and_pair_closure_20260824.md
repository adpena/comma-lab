## 3. PART 1 — the last pair, adjudicated at the demanded scale, adversarially

The pair demands **33.29% of each axis** — 42,382 B off a combined 127,292 B pool. dg2 measured two
rungs that shed 942 B and 1,576 B. That is a **26.9× lever arm** beyond the measured bracket.

### 3.1 I concede the fit, and then do not need it

| quantity | value |
|---|---:|
| bracket | [942, 1576] B, ratio 1.6730 |
| damage exponent | **0.7252** |
| credit exponent | **1.0000** (exact — rate is linear in bytes by construction) |
| **degrees of freedom** | **0** |
| bracket span in log-space | 0.5146 nats |
| extrapolation span | 3.2917 nats |
| **extrapolation ÷ bracket** | **6.40 bracket-lengths** |
| point estimate at 42,382 B | 278.2× |

Two points determine a power law exactly. There is no residual, so there is no goodness-of-fit and
no data-derived uncertainty. The exponent is an **interpolation coefficient over a 1.67× bracket**,
pushed 6.40 bracket-lengths out. Anyone quoting "278×" as a measurement is over-reading it, and I
wrote that number, so I say it first.

**So I discard it and bound the pair without any fit.** The k-ladder edit sets are **NESTED** —
ld1:9 says "six **nested** ... fields" and ld1:63–65 describes one descending cost ranking with a
deterministic tie-break, sliced at increasing k. Each edit converts a **correct** Lane label to an
incorrect Road label, so transmitted error is monotone in k *by construction*, not by inference.

Under monotone damage — the weakest assumption that survives that structure:

```
damage(42,382 B) >= damage(1,576 B)  = 0.721209 S
credit(42,382 B)                      = 0.028220 S
ratio                                >= 25.56x
```

**Even if the damage SATURATED completely — exponent 0, dropping 26.9× more of the field costing no
more distortion at all — the pair still refuses at 25.56×.** To clear, the damage exponent would
have to be **−0.985**: damage would have to *fall* as more of the field is dropped. That is not a
different magnitude; it is the **opposite sign**, and it is physically incoherent for a nested
correct→incorrect edit ladder.

**The verdict does not depend on the fit.** That is the honest form of the answer.

## 11. What this means, plainly — and the class of object that could clear

Three arms with disjoint methods now converge on the same statement, and this one closes the last
composition route: **dx2's object is exhausted as a composition problem.** The campaign does not
need a different combination of dx2's axes.

**Whether it needs a different OBJECT is now contingent, and §7 rank 2 decides it.** §6.1 separates
three mechanisms, and only one of them is a property of the representation:

- **rate slack** is exhausted because rate *was* optimized under a mandatory penalty. No retrain
  recovers it. This is representation-bound.
- **pose fragility** is a property of the **objective**, not the representation. An S-trained body
  could plausibly cheapen the pose-dominated refusals — carrier (100%), semantic (65.8–89.8%),
  dg2's field edits (93.3%), w72's renderer (65.3%) — **on this very representation**.
- **decode integrity** (HPAC, residual) is bound by exact-reconstruction, and no objective change
  touches it.

So: **fire rank 2 before committing a campaign to a new object.** If pose-inclusive training
materially cheapens ap1 `carrier_l1`, the new-object route is premature and the tier-3 bytes are
partly reachable here. If it does not, E2′ is refuted, E1 stands alone, and the class below is the
answer.

**The class, if it comes to that.** I am not designing one — that is another arm's charter. But the
measured record constrains the CLASS, and each property below is forced by a specific refusal:

1. **The address must be implicit, not a payload.** tba1 measured Gini **0.9951** with the top 1% of
   positions carrying **96.32%** of the bits, and `#1234` measured that the bit mass and the task
   mass occupy the **same** 0.2% of positions. Naming any subset costs more than it holds
   (tba1's best net over every threshold: **+9.45 B**; PR101 colex-rank: **+9.90 B**). bl1
   sharpens the target: **Lane is 0.59% of area but 33.56% of model bits — 90.1% of the whole
   demand.** The new object's units must have positions **entailed** by the units already sent, so
   the expensive class is **generated** rather than addressed.
2. **Rate must be changeable without rewriting the scored frames.** This is the property dg2's
   measurement forces, and it postdates sy2's naming. On dx2 the field drives the renderer and
   PoseNet scores the frames, so **no rate move is frame-neutral**: dg2's diagonal took `d_pose` to
   **344×** base and pose was **93.3–93.4%** of the damage. A new object must separate its
   rate-bearing structure from its frame-bearing structure, or it inherits this exact wall.
3. **Movable mass must be structural, not residual.** dx2's measured movable mass is **1,089 B**
   against a 42,382 B demand — 38.91× short — because the object converged, and everything that
   converged became load-bearing. An object born near the target size has movable mass by
   construction rather than as the leftovers of a search.

Properties 1 and 3 restate sy2's rank-3 naming — *"a born-small implicit evaluator-cell carrier,
not RC1/NR1's temporal-program codebook and not WS1's explicit worldsheet"* — reached here from the
triple and exhaustion arithmetic instead of from causal structure. That is corroboration, and I
credit sy2 for the naming. **Property 2 is the addition**, and it is the one dg2 bought.

**Where the next wave should aim:** fire **R3** first — $0, scorer-free, zero distortion risk, and
