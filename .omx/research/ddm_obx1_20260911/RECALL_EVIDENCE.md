   born-small body and specified a nonlinear learned carrier. The later HV3/HV4 custody adjudication
   closes that exact HG1 + inherited-DX2-carrier route and its downstream screen. This prevents OBX1
   from relabeling a one-hidden-layer exact-delta regressor as a new design. The live design must be
   co-trained in image space on the QBF object and must change the receiver-consumed representation.
2. TB1 retained two full-n600 Seg-only render measurements, but its 534,597/549,927 B totals are
   component estimates, not byte-closed archives, and Pose was not measured. They are inventory
   evidence, not cross-feasible objects.
3. The canonical `lane_band_camera_frame_rd_rate_v1` law contains a 41,526 B n600 real-code anchor
   for LBND2 and a 26,179 B Shannon floor. LB1's 2,832 B is an n32 nonconsecutive-selection payload,
   so it cannot be booked as an n600 carrier price. This forced the new falsifier to code its own
   exact support and values.
4. `procedural_predictor_plus_residual_correction_savings_v1` has two measured rate regressions when
   residual bytes were priced honestly. This rules out crediting free receiver code as though it
   made the video-derived correction free.
5. `score_marginal_lagrange_multipliers_v1`, `scorer_conditional_joint_rate_distortion_floor_v1`,
   `waterfill_annulus_through_r_store_realization_vs_witness_capacity_v1`, and the current Lane-band
   laws require own-object, realized-through-R distortion and actual coded bytes. They changed all
   three design descriptions from point predictions to explicit packet and distortion admission

6. The shared-assumption matrix contains 18 assumptions, not a shorter vehicle-local subset. The
   main memo profiles every one and distinguishes forced evaluator contracts from empirical forks.
7. Primary literature supplies mechanism anchors, not byte or distortion credit: LIIF for local
   coordinate-conditioned features; SHACIRA for compressed multiresolution hash features; Poisson

   residual state.

### What changed in the plan

- The best design is a jointly trained, multiresolution edge-local correction lattice that replaces
  part of QBF's latent section. It is not BS3's deferred exact-delta regressor and not a post-hoc
  Lane-band overlay.
- The `$0` falsifier uses decoded int8 RGB correction values at the exact shipped Lane-edge support,
  codes them with Brotli q11, applies them at the camera receiver before the frozen scorer, and
  retains every chunk. It does not borrow LB1, GDC3, or pointer-token prices.
- Designs A and C replace the existing latent budget instead of stacking a carrier on the 121,928 B
  selection estimate. Design B is the only additive form and is therefore capped at 15,394 B above
  the physical 106,606 B packet before ZIP effects.
- The prior HG1/BS3 learned-carrier continuation remains closed at its exact parent. The new burn
  specification reopens only the QBF image-space, jointly trained formulation and requires an
  explicit operator GO.
