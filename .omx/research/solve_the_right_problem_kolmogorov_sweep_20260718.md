## Canonical right-level coordinate/quotient law

Let the counted witness state be

```text
σ = (M, ξ, e, Tq)
```

where `M` is the class/cell Morse–Smale complex, `ξ` is the temporal twist, `e` contains irreducible
gauge/birth/death/light events, and `Tq` is the explicit class/cell stationary-metamer quotient residual.
For a generic deterministic decoder `G`, every video-derived seed, predictor coefficient, table, and learned
weight is counted:

```text
σ* = argmin_σ [
  100*d_seg(argmax SegNet(A R(G(σ))_1), P*)
  + sqrt(10*d_pose(PoseNet(Y A R(G(σ))_0:1)[:6], z*))
  + 25*|Encode(σ)|/N
].
```

The temporal state obeys the unique-home decomposition

```text
M_(t+1) = H(ξ_t) M_t ⊕ gauge_t ⊕ event_t,
```

where only the `H(ξ_t)`-predictable component is free. The remainder is counted exactly once. Spatial
class-pair control may use the frozen head normal `a_(c,c') = W_c-W_c'`, but only after composing it with the
*actual* witness/render/resize/scorer pullback and integer feasible set. No isomorphism between present witness
FiLM channels and frozen SegNet head channels is assumed. Projection is local sequential convexification with
trust regions and realized-through-`R` monotone acceptance; rank four alone does not prove a global convex
preimage or global Dykstra convergence.

Operational corollary:

```text
represent at the right level
→ change to evaluator coordinates
→ solve/project the legal local preimage
→ train the quotient residual
→ encode only irreducible seed
→ select on receiver-closed exact S.
```
