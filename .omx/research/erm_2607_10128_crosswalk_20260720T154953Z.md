6–8 explicitly construct task-specific memories and rule-distance potentials; Table 6 reports no learned
selector parameters. The learned Energy-Transformer branch is an appendix motivation on a weaker Sudoku
checkpoint. Therefore the honest comparison is:

- ERM mainline: an explicit, task-engineered structured verifier over candidate outputs;
- Pact: exact frozen-scorer geometry plus an exact final evaluator. The rank-4 head margin law is exact in

- consequence: ERM may improve **which candidates receive exact evaluation**. It cannot replace exact
  through-R acceptance or establish a contest score.

## 3. MINE-ABLE 1 — energy-ranked candidate batching for #396/#400 and #319

### 3.1 Ranking energy

For candidate `c`, target class `y*_p`, and rivals `j`, define predicted margins

`mhat_pj(c) = m_pj(base) + Delta_m_pj(c)`.

`m_pj(base)` is the frozen scorer margin. `Delta_m` must come from the registered corrected realization
path (`flip_margin_step_law_v1`: first-order + secant + bounded QP), not the known-naive first-order form.
The exact head identity applies once the penultimate feature is known; the RGB/pixel pullback is only a
prediction and is labeled as such.

Rank with the target-debt Cole–Hopf potential

`E_seg(c) = sum_p tau * log(1 + sum_{j != y*_p} exp(-mhat_pj(c)/tau))`,

restricted to candidate-affected cells plus the measured nonlocal collateral support. Add exact candidate
archive bytes when available and the existing ξ-conditioned Pose proxy only as a ranking term. The final
ordering key is `(E_seg, predicted_pose_debt, exact_archive_bytes, candidate_id)`. It is never called a
score. Fresh exact through-R cells and complete archive bytes remain the sole accept/rollback authority.

This uses what is exact honestly:

- **MEASURED #542 target surface:** all `117,964,800` n600 scorer pixels processed in `2.5133 s`; mean
  top-one Gibbs probability `0.9958448`; `2.6664%` below `0.99`; `2,629` fp16-logit/cache argmax
  differences (`2.2286e-5`). This validates a cheap **target builder**, not an inverse-solve driver.
- **MEASURED/DERIVED frozen head:** centered rank `4`; pairwise feature-space flip distance is exactly
  `|margin|/||Delta w||`. Pixel-space pullback is first-order unless the corrected inner Jacobian is
  measured.
- **Exact final authority:** #400's `DirectionPinnedPairLocalObjective` recomposes n-pair `d_seg`, nonlinear
  Pose, and candidate-specific archive bytes; strict exact-S monotonicity remains the gate.

## 4. MINE-ABLE 2 — parallel tempering on the uint8 preimage lattice

### 4.1 What PT cannot improve

`solve_bounded_integer_block` performs bounded, gcd-pruned DFS over at most four uint8 taps. A completed
search is an IFF feasibility result for that affine block. PT cannot make that proof more exact, and a PT
failure can never prove infeasibility. Therefore the affine solver stays unchanged and
`FEASIBLE_EXACT`/`INFEASIBLE_EXHAUSTIVE` retain authority.

### 4.2 Where a probe is legitimate

The nonlinear hard-oracle repair is deterministic greedy `+/-1` descent. It admits only fresh hard-oracle
improvement, but its negative statuses are explicitly unknown. Local basins are possible there. The minimal
probe is named `probe_uint8_preimage_parallel_tempering_v1`:

1. Input only the smallest preregistered real-cell stratum for which deterministic repair returns
   `STALLED_UNKNOWN`, `CYCLE_DETECTED_UNKNOWN`, or `BUDGET_EXHAUSTED_UNKNOWN`. If no such cell exists, emit
   `N-A_NO_STALLED_INSTANCE` and stop.
2. Initialize four replicas from the same exact affine-feasible preimage. Proposals are integer nullspace
   moves that preserve the affine numerator and uint8 bounds; malformed/non-preserving moves are rejected
   before energy evaluation.
3. Normalize the cheap margin/Cole–Hopf debt by its observed robust spread and use four derived temperatures
   `(0.25, 0.5, 1, 2)` times that spread. Use a fixed seed. Four replicas times 16 proposals gives the same
   `64` proposal budget as one default hard-repair iteration. A zero, non-finite, or otherwise undefined
   robust spread emits `N-A_DEGENERATE_ENERGY_SPREAD` and stops rather than inventing a temperature scale.
4. Neighbor swaps use the paper's Metropolis ratio. Cheap energy guides exploration only. Fresh decoded-
   uint8 hard-oracle evaluation ranks terminal replica candidates and is the only `HARD_ACCEPT`.
5. Compare with deterministic repair under identical proposal and hard-oracle-call budgets. PT survives
   only if it strictly improves the exact hard-debt key, or reaches zero violations with fewer exact oracle
   calls, without worse projection/rate custody. A tie is “nothing to adopt.”

This is a formulation-level probe for nonlinear repair, not a family claim about preimages and not a route
around the measured EV waterfill. Candidate cells remain ordered by Fisher/margin EV, corrected
inner-Jacobian realization, Road–Lane necessity, and rate break-even.

## 6. PDW2/R1 recursion crosswalk

ERM's diagnostic separation is useful:

1. **generator oracle:** did the candidate pool contain a receiver-realizable good state?
2. **selector oracle:** did cheap energy retain the exact best state?
3. **realization oracle:** did counted bytes expand through scorer-free RGB, uint8, resize, and frozen cells?

R1/#576 currently fails at layer 3 before a meaningful recursive schedule exists. The sealed 138-byte PDW2
packet is consumed and maps an externally supplied rank-4 quotient field to labels, but the necessary
spatial generator and scorer-free RGB/camera pullback are absent (`PDW2_COEFFICIENT_ONLY_SPATIAL_NONIDENTIFIABILITY`).
Thus ERM structurally validates the need to separate generation, selection, and realization. It supplies
no transferable receiver recursion depth, temperature ladder, or stopping threshold.
