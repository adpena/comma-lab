## Recursive-fractal composition law

Task503 consumes canonical equation **`fullstack_unique_home_assignment_v1`** and registers no replacement.

Let `chi_I + chi_A = 1` be a deterministic partition of unity between smooth interior and boundary annulus, let `phi_k` be interior atoms, `psi_j` the one selected localized boundary basis, and let `Gamma`, `r_tie`, `xi`, `p6`, and `c_chroma` be the counted task-coordinate packet. At epoch/stage `e`, the representation is the **DERIVED** object

```text
C_e = Q_e[
  chi_I * sum_k a_k phi_k + chi_A * sum_j b_j psi_j ;
  Gamma_partition, r_tie, xi, p6, c_chroma
]

W_e = R o G_h(C_e)

C^(ell+1) = Q_ell(Glue_xi_ell({C_i^(ell)}) + r_ell)
```

`Q` is deterministic canonical quantization/packing, `G_h` is a hash-bound generic renderer/decoder, and `R` is the exact scorer-boundary resize/round/uint8 realization. `Glue_xi` transports child packets. `r_ell` is admitted only when a receiver-closed A/B proves the lower-level state cannot reproduce the scored behavior at lower counted bytes.

The composition has five binding rules:

1. **Partition of unity:** smooth interior and localized annulus overlap only through `chi_I + chi_A = 1`; no pixel is unowned or doubly charged.
2. **Generators, not boundaries:** per-class Laguerre/level-set generator coordinates produce boundaries; sampled dense boundaries are not the native stored object.
3. **Unique home:** each scored degree of freedom is encoded once at its lowest sufficient level. Parent packets carry transport and receiver-proven residuals, not copies.
4. **RGB only at the scorer boundary:** the stored representation is task state. The legal deterministic receiver materializes RGB because the frozen scorers require it.
5. **Epoch annealing:** `Q_e`, the active basis, and the local metric may change only at typed, checkpointed stage boundaries; every stage checkpoint preserves enough state for byte-close replay.

“Fractal” means the same partition → transport → sparse correction → pack → parse-back → realize sequence recurs across pixel/class/boundary/frame/pair/scale. It is not a claim that the source video is statistically self-similar.
