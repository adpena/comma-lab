## GESTALT-DELTA

The gestalt held the born loss was mis-calibrated *across the boundary*. It is not — at any fixed
temperature it tracks the argmax faithfully. What is broken is that the objective's **units move
during training**: the schedule deflates the loss 8.4× harder than the field's own signal pushes it,
so a monotone-falling loss is compatible with a monotone-worsening field. This is `[[m157]]`
(solve the objective on the shipping axis) at the **TIME** axis: the reported quantity was not
comparable to its own earlier values. **Any annealed surrogate must be reported at a fixed reference
temperature, or its time series is not a time series of anything.** Second delta: `[[m131]]` gains a
mechanism — the Lane/Movable pressure here is one-sided by construction, and its over-paint peak is
co-located with the d_seg peak, so the rare-class axis and the excursion axis are one axis.
