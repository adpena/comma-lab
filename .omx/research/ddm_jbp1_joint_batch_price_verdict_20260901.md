## Why B1-B3 are blocked, not zero-priced

All 3/3 SFP1 proposal schemas say `refit_required: true` and name
`refit_cross_group_causal_schedule`. None declares an implementation, trained model, decoder
source, schedule source, model payload, or model bytes. RXC1/JG2 consumes none of that operation;
it pins the shipped 13,515 B HPAC section at sha
`602115b323b0e403d08287af9b273a2d4fb23e026d83c1f6e4609ed77ef98f98` and replaces only the
token RC64 tail under the shipped group plan.

Prior source-verified evidence makes this absence load-bearing: in the shipped HPAC family the
cross-group index is simultaneously the coding partition and the causal mask baked into the
trained convolution weights. A different cross-group schedule therefore requires a different
trained model. Re-encoding only the changed X fields under fixed shipped G/M would measure a
different mechanism and cannot be reported as the chartered joint G/M price.
