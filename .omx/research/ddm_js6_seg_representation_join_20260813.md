## What was built

Each proposal joins real landed objects rather than stand-ins:

1. **MEASURED target coordinate:** one 8-connected component of a directed CP135→GT argmax error
   edge from the retained full-n600 fields.
2. **DERIVED current-base executable coordinate:** every component pixel maps to its nearest CP135
   semantic token of the same source class. There is no radius, shared generic template, inherited
   sample, or generic coefficient basis. Single-token rows are excluded, directly respecting EC3's
   later singleton closure.
3. **MEASURED counted grammar section:** the exact component is passed through the landed G1
   `EVENT/CENTROID/SHAPE` worldsheet encoder and byte-canonical decoder. The representation error
   against the source component is recorded rather than hidden.
4. **MEASURED receiver section:** the derived token coordinates are encoded through the landed EC1
   event packet, decoded, applied jointly to the real CP135 semantic-token plane, and replayed through
   R and uint8.
5. **MEASURED retained evidence:** raw/Brotli-q11/LZMA9-extreme joined packets, exact and G1-decoded
   masks, candidate tokens, pre-R tensor, base/candidate camera tensors, base/candidate scorer inputs,
   scorer-input delta, and uint8-quantum support are stored with byte counts and SHA-256. Every
   materialized float32 tensor is retained exactly; float16 copies are additional compact consumers,
   not replacements.
