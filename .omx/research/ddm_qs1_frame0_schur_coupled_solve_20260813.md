## Solver and stopping rule

The solve uses CP135's own `600 × 12` signed-int12 carrier coordinates, basis, coefficients, and renderer.
For each retained frame-1 edit it measures the six-dimensional PoseNet leakage, forms the exact receiver-
realized `6 × 12` frame-0 Jacobian with `±1` lattice differences, and adapts the banked PR135
Gauss-Newton form (`damping=0.01`, maximum float step `32`). It then exhausts a radius-two integer cube in
the three strongest dimensions and runs signed-int12 coordinate descent until one complete pass accepts no
strict improvement. The fourteen rows required one to seven full passes; no arbitrary iteration cap chose
the result.

The conservative Pose screen bounds the population d_pose increase by Cauchy on the exact residual and the
retained CP135-versus-GT error vector, then uses the locally declared `603 S / d_pose` calibration. Exact
local one-pair dpose deltas are retained as diagnostics but are not used to weaken that bound.
