## Seven defect classes — instances, cures, and owners

### C1 — scope escalation: census or formulation becomes family/law/theorem

**Instances.** WS0/MAIN's four-instance law; DX2/hot state's `remaining rate progress ZERO by r012
theorem`; DJ1's family headline over a carrier-only fixed-seg/fixed-rate bound; GS2/JO1's additive
and deblur family prose; GS3's single-survivor/theorem phrasing; SR2F's continuous-family close from

`INSTANCE < FORMULATION < FAMILY < PARADIGM`. R012 becomes “all inventoried mutually composable
current-body rungs in the 2026-08-21 census are harvested.” WS0 becomes “horizontal-row ordinal
worldsheet formulation loses.” DJ1 becomes “carrier-only CPU-declaration formulation is
strategically dominated.” SR2F becomes “tested alpha ladder on hv1 and rc2 loses.”

**Owner.** MAIN for hot state and routing prose; DX2, DJ1, GS2/GS3, JO1, and SR2F for their next
append-only supersession.

### C2 — unmatched-object or small-sample transfer controls a full decision

**Instances.** DC1's five-group prototype; IG1 L1-L4's cross-object causal decomposition; WC2's
three-pair 3.627303x factor in a 1,800-pair ETA; WS1's semantic subgrammar seeds before per-cell
races; SR2F's omitted small-alpha region.

**Cure.** Preserve small samples as mechanism proofs only. A decision row needs the same object,
receiver, coder, axis, and represented quantity. For WC2, use a stratified current-stage slice and
an empirical rate distribution; for WS1, race generic curve versus specialized grammar on each
stratum, then full n600; for IG1, measure implicit/optimal-grammar/hybrid on one source object rather
than comparing archive components with different models.

**Owner.** WC2/MAIN, WS1, IG1, and the relevant formulation owner. DC1S has already completed the
DC1 cure.

### C3 — generic scientific/economic thresholds do control work

**Instances.** ES1's 137,500 and stream allocations; WS0/WS1 130K/110K; DC1 3 KiB; GS2's 5-10x
discount; EC2P/JO1's 10x LIVE band; R012/GS3/WD4's 1.5x checkpoint bar; PW2's 5% deblur threshold;
FC3's `abs Spearman <0.5`; WC2's 2x swap-cost margin and one-hour stage boxes.

**Cure.** Separate exact boundaries from operating policies. Exact score/byte break-even is derived
from the complete candidate. Uncertainty margins come from repeated matched measurements. Compute
triage comes from expected value versus actual wall/dollar cost. When none exists, label a constant
`GENERIC-PLANNING-PRIOR` and forbid it from licensing a family verdict.

**Owner.** Each producing arm; MAIN owns the charter-time constants and their consuming hot-state
lines.

### C4 — generic resource constants drift away from the workload they guard

JO3/JO4/JO5's 16 GiB RSS, 48 GiB projection/reserve, 44 GiB minimum free, and 72 h wall; WC2's

**Cure.** Generate the guard values from the retained workload manifest: measured peak plus named

not an arbitrary age, identify a self-owned active claim. Fail closed if the derivation receipt is
missing.

**Owner.** AD1/MAIN for claims; JO5/MAIN r9 sealer for the live solve lineage; WC2 for wall budgets.
