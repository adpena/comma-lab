# Shared receiver R1 build spec

Build one fail-closed, content-addressed admission surface for the PDW2 coefficient consumer and the PDW1 realization leg. It must prove what the current receiver actually consumes and price every available spatial/preimage representation against the `477.8 B/pair` and `d_seg <= 3.39e-4` gate. R1 must not emit an in-box row: a canonical contest-CPU evaluator invocation and trusted production-receiver parser are not wired.

## Constraints

- `$0` local only; no paid dispatch, long training, pointer mutation, or promotion.
- Reuse the settled PDW2 native-fp32 receiver, production factor-2 receiver, exact score law, and registered Fisher/resize/preimage laws. Do not rebuild them.
- Treat `bounded_uint8_resize_preimage_cell_feasibility_v1` honestly: its executable evaluator proves affine integer feasibility only; SegNet `HARD_ACCEPT` is a separate encode-side oracle predicate.
- Count the PDW2 packet only when a scorer-free spatial/RGB consumer exists and packet mutation changes the decoded result. Target-only or quotient-label-only evidence keeps `through_r_authority=false`.
- The dense quotient field, PDW1 label/fill stream, and EV-ranked sparse repair are distinct formulations. Do not compose their gains or infer one formulation's distortion for another.
- A negative is formulation-scoped and must name the untested optimal form: a counted curvelet/shearlet or equivalent compact spatial generator jointly solved with the active-set hard-oracle preimage and `xi` Pose factor.

## Acceptance

1. Strict schema, finite-number, pair-count, hash, and cross-receipt consistency checks reject stale or type-laundered evidence.
2. The result reports measured/derived labels separately for packet bytes, dense spatial-field bytes, PDW1 carrier bytes, d_seg, hard-oracle residual classes, sparse-repair marginal value, and archive gate.
3. `success` remains unconditionally false in R1. Caller JSON and self-asserted booleans are inadmissible; a later enabling change must derive the n600 archive, production parse-back, through-R contest-CPU row, packet causality, content hashes, sample count, and rate/distortion gates from trusted replay.
4. Existing receipts produce a durable formulation-scoped blocker with the exact dominant cells/term; no null metric is coerced to zero.
5. Ruff, `py_compile`, and focused pytest pass; changed Python files receive two clean review-tracker passes before serializer commit.
