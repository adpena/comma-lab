Produce a source-cited, ranked inventory of throughput, wall-clock, and
convergence negatives whose historical wording could be read more broadly than
the tested implementation supports.  Regrade each claim on

## Row contract

Each distinct verdict row must state: source and exact historical claim; tested
form; specific naivete or evidence that the form was optimal; corrected verdict
scope; untested optimal-form reformulation; evidence authority; expected-value
rank; `$0` testability; reactivation criterion; and current owner or hold.

No non-n600, advisory, proxy, disengaged, unbuilt, or first-cut result may close
a family.  Missing backend/data/device custody is a runtime or custody blocker,
not a technique verdict.  A result that used the best-known form with complete
authority may stand, but only at the narrowest supported level.
