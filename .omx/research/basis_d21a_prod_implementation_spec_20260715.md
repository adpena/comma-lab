## Objective

Close three independent P0 build obligations without weakening V9 provenance:

1. register a genuine-frame three-arm V9 A/B/C family whose sole scientific
   treatment is one typed `BasisLeverSpec` selecting polar directional Fourier,
   finite windowed curvelet, or compact shearlet;
2. consume the already-proved D21a blind-coordinate transform in the real
   byte-close/inflate path, gated by an authenticated n600 zero-delta receipt;
3. re-derive and behaviorally verify the already-landed D-A/D-B telemetry
   producers and repair only demonstrated gaps.

All new negative statements must carry a verdict scope. Families stay OPEN and
the genuine-frame launch ticket stays PREPARED_NOT_FIRED.

## Non-negotiable implementation rules

- The trainer parser is the executable flag authority. Never invent a flag and
  never use `--dsl-lever`.
- Every emitted scientific value has a typed DSL owner, LawRef/equation ID,
  executable trainer consumer, generated-inflate consumer where relevant, and
  receipt edge. A label or manifest-only row is insufficient.
- Source/declaration closure is fail-closed. The historical expected/live
  prefixes `6cfa9845`/`5c926130` are evidence of a stale seal, not values to
  preserve. Build a new literal expected declaration digest from a reviewed,
  canonical declaration payload and test that mutation refuses. Do not derive
  the expected digest from live bytes at validation time.
- Reuse `BasisLeverSpec` and existing `windowed_curvelet_frame`,
  `compact_shearlet_frame`, `v9_provenance_gates`, and Bregman information-
  geometry functions. Do not build parallel basis, metric, or provenance
  systems.
- The Fourier arm remains the governed A/B control. Do not promote it as the
  default. Curvelet/shearlet receiver results are macOS-CPU advisory,
  non-byte-equal, and non-promotable; do not encode their ranks as selection
  authority.
- Any FFT/DCT/rFFT used only as a measurement tool must retain an honest same-
  line `# FFT_TOOL_USE_OK:<rationale>` waiver. Do not add executable Fourier
  basis construction outside the explicit legacy control.
- New state is additive and resume-safe. Existing checkpoint formats and
  default-off/default-compatible behavior must remain loadable.
- Commit only in the isolated `codexwt/basis_d21a_prod_20260715_20260715T092624Z`
  worktree via the serializer. MAIN remains the review-and-merge boundary.

  claim a measured live latent covariance or a family verdict.
