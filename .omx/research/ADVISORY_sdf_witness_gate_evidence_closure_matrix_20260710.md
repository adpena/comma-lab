### 16.2 Signal and information allocation

\[
\Delta S = 100\Delta d_{seg}
  + \sqrt{10d'_{pose}}-\sqrt{10d_{pose}}

\]

1.273 bytes absent Pose effects. This is a local marginal budget, not permission to ignore the

