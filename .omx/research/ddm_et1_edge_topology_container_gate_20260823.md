## Mechanism identity

The new container stores a shared binary space-time topology. Every homogeneous leaf is a class-owned
generator that fills an implicit evaluator cell. The deterministic split policy supplies coordinates;
the payload stores neither boundary samples nor a dense per-cell class array. The five class carriers
share one topology, so a derived inter-class edge has one home.

This satisfies the charter's three required mechanism properties:

1. generators, not sampled boundaries;
2. all five classes have class-owned generator leaves;
3. evaluator cells are implicit in the deterministic receiver traversal.

It is not a boundary list, a single-class fit, or an explicit-cell container, and it is not labelled a
TOY-BRACKET. It is a real exact current-object formulation with an encoder, a decoder, real coder
payloads, deterministic repeats, and complete archive parse-back. Its narrowness is the generator
vocabulary: fixed midpoint BSP cells rather than V8's heterogeneous analytic generator vocabulary.
