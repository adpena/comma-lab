### Ledger eligibility boundary

T1–T7 are admissible teacher surfaces because the receipt shows that the target or scorer behavior exists and the closed defect is storage, student family, or realization. T8–T10 are not additional “big-rate wins”: they are method/constraint controls that prevent NR1 from repeating stale compensation, collateral-blind targeting, or post-hoc pose. This distinction prevents a teacher ledger from turning into a generic list of every good mechanism.

The charter's phrase that HPAC, PP1, SP1, and WS1 “carry the partition perfectly” must be read by object type. PP1/WS1 carry a partition target; SP1 carries correction support; HPAC losslessly carries its own decoded semantic token object, whose exact DX2 row still has nonzero `d_seg=0.00020139`. Semantic equality is not end-to-end zero distortion.
