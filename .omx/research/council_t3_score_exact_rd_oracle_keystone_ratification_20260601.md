## VERDICT: PROCEED_WITH_REVISIONS

The keystone thesis **survives adversarial challenge in its CO-EQUAL-NECESSITY form** and is **FALSIFIED in its singular form.** The differentiable score-exact oracle IS the unifying objective that makes the seven layers composable through one Lagrangian `L = 100·d̃_seg + √(10·d̃_pose) + 25·R/N` — that is real, verified bit-exact, and the correct meta-Lagrangian instantiation. But the empirical record (Z8) is dispositive that an RD-optimal allocator fed an oracle gradient is NECESSARY-NOT-SUFFICIENT: the substrate's achievable R(D) is a co-equal binding constraint. PROCEED on the unified-objective architecture; REVISE the framing + gate three named risks before any paid dispatch.

## The single most important adversarial finding (Contrarian + Assumption-Adversary, VERIFIED_VIA_EMPIRICAL_ANCHOR)

The design's own §3 table dismisses "representation R(D)" as *"necessary, not sufficient."* The Z8 empirical record proves the **oracle is equally necessary-not-sufficient**:

- `z8_joint_p18_p19_deadzone_rate_attack` (MEMORY): the `joint_p18_p19_waterfill` allocator — fed exactly the kind of oracle-gradient saliency (`s_seg` DeepFool + `s_pose` Fisher) this design proposes — was the **RD-optimal trade at EVERY keep ratio** (joint beat magnitude at every keep; J−mag score −0.049/−0.014/−0.841), **and Z8 STILL sat ~24× from frontier**. Verbatim verdict: *"dead-zone cannot close structural gap; raw-float32 detail storage is the rate killer NOT the mask."*
- `z8_600pair_byte_closed_contest_score` (MEMORY): 104.94 [advisory], RATE-binding 97%, **546× from frontier**.
- `z8_detail_entropy_headroom` (MEMORY): wavelet blob ~99.5% of archive = THE rate lever, 1000–4000× above the per-subband Shannon floor; **lossless byteshuffle is a DEAD END** — the win REQUIRES quantize+entropy-code, which the substrate's representation R(D) governs.

**Translation:** the oracle pointed perfectly at the right bytes; the substrate could not represent a boundary cheaply enough for the pointing to change the score. A keystone that determines WHERE to spend bits, on a substrate that cannot make the spend cheap, is half a stack. This is not a refutation of the design — it is the missing co-keystone the design under-weights.
