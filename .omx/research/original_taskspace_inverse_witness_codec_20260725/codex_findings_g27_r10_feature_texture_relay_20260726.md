## The structural composition

The relay composes original production primitives rather than inventing another
carrier:

- canonical XIP2 owns the counted per-pair SE(3) trajectory;
- NumPy-fp64 ground-homography warp transports frame 0;
- strict DASH1 owns chronological phase/events and supplies the counted spatial
  pullback for local texture;
- optional stratified affine flow is admitted only beneath a counted polygon;
- integer luma, Laplacian/edge, feature, channel projection, and oriented phase
  texture finish in actual uint8 output.

The PDW2 nonidentifiability lesson is now executable policy: texture
coefficients without DASH1 refuse, flow coefficients without counted polygon
support refuse, and base-feature operands bind an exact upstream realized-pair
SHA. There is no coefficient-only fallback.

## Asymmetric codec contract

The adapter distinguishes all three placement surfaces:

1. **Unbounded encode-only compiler:** source analysis, maximum inverse solve,
   operand fitting/selection, scorer/costate work, and the final joint-descent
   linker.
2. **Generic bounded decode VM:** strict parse, XIP2/DASH1 decode, SE(3) and
   stratified warps, polygon rasterization, integer luma/edge/feature/texture,
   and uint8 round/clip.
3. **Counted video-specific state:** all nine physical packet sections and
   their exact ranges. The current learned irreducible-residual section list is
   empty. Training may populate a later counted residual only after maximum
   inverse solving leaves measured debt.

Joint descent is not treated as a representation family. It remains the final
exact realized-score and complete-ZIP linker pass.
