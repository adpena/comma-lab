## 2. Gestalt: one quotient-space codec, not independent gates

Let `P` be the counted ep725 predictor program, `Z` its semantic field after the chosen topology action, `Y1` its realized camera frame after optional same-class realization, and `Y0=A(Y1)` the chronological pose preimage. The evaluator sees only

```text
Q = (argmax SegNet(R(Y1)), PoseNet(R(Y0), R(Y1))[:6], exact archive.zip bytes).
```

The production problem is therefore one constrained sublevel search:

```text
min  S(P,G,Rg,A) = 100*d_seg + sqrt(10*d_pose) + 25*B/37_545_489
subject to exact receiver, source-lineage, and archive-byte closure.
```

There is no authoritative target value for Seg, Pose, or rate independently. At any exact archive size `B`, pose distortion `d_pose`, and live competitive target `S*`, the largest jointly admissible Seg distortion is the derived quantity

```text
d_seg_max(B,d_pose,S*) = (S* - sqrt(10*d_pose) - 25*B/37_545_489) / 100.
```

If the right side is negative, that pose/rate state cannot cross the target even at zero Seg debt. This is the operational form of the user's “partial/differential equation” observation: every coordinate budget is conditional on the realized other coordinates. Fixed per-axis gates are forbidden.

### Solution-description primacy

The charged object is not “the inverse problem” in the abstract. It is the counted sufficient statistic that causes the legal generic receiver to reproduce one selected evaluator-equivalent solution. Let `D` denote the exact generic `inflate.py`/receiver program and let `[Y]_Q` be the set of received uint8 frame populations with the accepted frozen-evaluator coordinates. G17's representation problem is operationally

```text
choose Y* in [Y]_Q and counted z such that D(z) = Y*,
then minimize the exact archive.zip bytes of z under the complete contest score.
```

This is a measured program-length objective, not a claim to compute Kolmogorov complexity. Older “store the problem, then solve it” formulations are only one candidate factorization. They are dominated whenever directly describing the selected quotient representative—or a smaller statistic from which the generic receiver reconstructs it—costs fewer exact archive bytes. A stored problem plus solve exceptions may compete only as a complete receiver-closed object whose exact total beats the best solution description; it has no conceptual priority.

The receiver may perform arbitrarily rich **generic, deterministic, legal** work within the runtime budget: regenerate fixed bases, reconstruct tensor/temporal factors, entropy-decode counted streams, fill gauge/null coordinates, run inverse realization or bounded search, and rasterize analytic topology. But every video-derived selector, parameter, atom, latent, exception, codebook, entropy-context choice, target-dependent branch, or seed selected because of the frozen video is counted in `archive.zip`. No scorer weights, targets, teacher states, oracle products, dense preimages, or target-dependent tables may be hidden in receiver code. A generic search that cannot choose the same solution from counted `z` without consulting those forbidden inputs is not a decoder.

This is an explicit compiler-placement rule: **everything not contest-video-specific belongs on the free generic receiver surface; the counted payload contains only the irreducible contest-video-specific sufficient statistic.** Every future representation freezes a `G17CompilerPlacementManifestV1` before byte claims. It classifies every logical field, table, seed, branch, and executable asset by provenance and physical home:

| class | lawful home | examples and exact rule |
|---|---|---|
| generic deterministic mechanism | `inflate.py` / `inflate.sh` or another rule-lawful free runtime surface | solver, synthesis/repair/projection loop, fixed basis generator, factor expansion, entropy decoder, gauge fill/selection algorithm, inverse realization, bounded search; it must be video-independent, deterministic, portable, and inside the 30-minute wall |
| irreducible video-specific statistic | counted `archive.zip` section | selected topology/events, factor values, fitted weights/deltas, latents, learned atoms/dictionaries, exceptions, target-selected seeds/branches, gauge-representative selectors, fitted entropy contexts; exact bytes and SHA-256 are mandatory |

### Population framing is not yet a population grammar

P-once population framing closes the catastrophic predictor-duplication bug, but it does **not** by itself make V1 an efficient population codec. `TACG17G1/A1` still concatenate up to 150 self-contained bounded nested payloads. Outer DEFLATE is measured and may discover incidental repeated bytes, but V1 has no explicit cross-shard dictionary, no shared topology/realization/pose atom table, no globally delta-coded chronological event stream, no deterministic cross-shard entropy context, and no population MDL allocator that can choose one shared atom plus sparse escapes instead of 150 locally repeated descriptions. Therefore G17 V1 is a lawful exact baseline and composition envelope, not a claim of rate sufficiency, population-optimal representation, or the final codec.

The load-bearing escalation edge is `G17_POPULATION_GLOBAL_GRAMMAR_IMPLEMENTATION_OWED`. It blocks any conclusion that the sharded V1 envelope exhausts population compression, but it does not justify changing the frozen V1 ABI without evidence. The exact bridge is:

### An additive envelope is not yet a substitutive codec

V1 holds the exact ep725 P immutable and adds G/A/terminal bytes. That is the right control for causal composition, but it can only spend new bytes around an existing `84,536`-byte allocation. It cannot reclaim a base neuron made redundant by a shared semantic atom, merge a repeated P.code trajectory into a global event stream, move pose-relevant degrees of freedom from P texture into A, or trade P precision against a counted residual. Therefore neither P-once nor a future additive global-grammar section may be called the final codec architecture.

The frontier child owes `G17_SUBSTITUTIVE_RATE_REALLOCATION_IMPLEMENTATION_OWED`: a separately versioned receiver/program whose candidate manifest exposes the exact counted partition `(P.manifest, P.base, P.code, P.pose, shared semantic/paint grammar, A, learned residual, entropy metadata)` and admits atomic actions from this closed proposal taxonomy:

- `PRUNE_DELETE`: physically remove a component and every now-dead decoder dependency;
- `MERGE_SHARE`: replace repeated components with one counted shared atom plus counted references;
- `REPLACE`: exchange one decoder/basis/grammar component for a different receiver-realized component;
- `MIGRATE`: move a degree of freedom between P.base, P.code, the population grammar, A, and the residual so it is counted exactly once; and
- `REQUANTIZE`: jointly change precision, scale, codebook, and entropy context for the surviving components.

An action earns no nominal byte credit. Every proposal builds the new exact member/archive bytes with the removed material genuinely absent, strict-parses under its own versioned receiver, double-replays all 600 pairs, and runs the actual frozen scorer. Its matched control differs only by the registered action or atomic bundle; selection uses complete `delta_S`, not parameter count, raw section sums, estimated entropy, local functional distance, or postulated replacement savings. Video-derived weights, dictionaries, codebooks, event streams, latents, and selectors remain counted. Generic decode algorithms remain free. HOPE-style prune/merge/macro bundles may schedule these proposals, but cannot populate distortion or rate.

The substitutive candidate DAG retains coherent macro bundles as well as their elementary actions and records each action's static pre-action parameter payoff only as proposal metadata. It must expose the exact before/after section byte vector and an ownership conservation receipt proving every surviving degree of freedom has one counted home. A deletion control, a matched-byte reallocation control, and a same-decoded-output repack control are mandatory. One failed action or bundle does not kill the taxonomy. This blocker does not prevent exact evaluation if an immutable-P V1 object already crosses the live target; it prevents misrepresenting an additive baseline as an exhausted or optimal codec.

`lambda` is a continuation coordinate, never an acceptance threshold and never a substitute for the actual contest coefficient. From a low-rate-pressure teacher neighborhood toward the contest coefficient, the compiler alternates: select a quotient/gauge representative; replace target-specific computation with generic receiver work; factor weights/codes across time and population; turn recurring geometry into shared topology/events; prune/delete; merge/share; migrate capacity between P/G/A/residual/entropy sections; requantize; and add a counted learned escape only when current receiver telemetry proves an analytic debt. At every knot it rebuilds a new exact complete object, physically removes any claimed deleted bytes, replays the current receiver twice, and measures current frozen-scorer outputs plus exact whole ZIP bytes through G7. The teacher may guide proposal order or distillation loss; it never supplies a distortion/rate field or an admission decision.

Because uint8 realization, argmax cells, gauge choice, and entropy coding make this path discontinuous and nonconvex, the homotopy retains nondominated states, immediate continuation neighbors, branch points, and coherent macro actions rather than one greedy scalar trace. HOPE-style static initial payoff can schedule a macro but cannot price it. A state advances only on actual current-object evidence, and final selection is the minimum complete contest score over the frozen measured coverage—not the minimum `J_lambda` at an intermediate knot.

Each proposed factor/action family owes the following current-object controls:

- a physical deletion control and an ownership-conservation receipt;
- a matched-byte reallocation control and, when possible, a same-decoded-output repack control;
- independent-versus-shared atom and tensor-factor controls across pair, time, class/stratum, and population axes;
- chronological event-order and temporal-permutation falsifiers;
- analytic-versus-learned residual controls with the learned escape charged in full;
- complete Seg-equivalent gauge alternatives carried through chronological A and selected by joint Pose plus rate;
- a teacher-free/current-G17 initialization control proving that any benefit is in the newly compiled candidate rather than imported historical state; and
- exact current-receiver/scorer deletion telemetry for every claimed atom, context, selector, and target-dependent branch.

This bridge feeds the population-global grammar, gauge scheduler, and substitutive allocation child; it is not a fourth additive sidecar. Its preferred output is the minimal counted sufficient statistic for the selected evaluator-equivalent solution. A counted stored-problem-plus-runtime-solve form remains eligible only if its complete exact total wins against direct selected-solution descriptions. All generic lawful solving, synthesis, factor expansion, projection, gauge fill, and inverse realization is pushed into the active receiver subject to determinism, portability, source-boundary, and 30-minute constraints; every contest-video-specific choice stays counted.

### Evaluator-equivalent gauge representative selection

For a fixed received semantic partition, many RGB/chroma/luma realizations lie in the same frozen-SegNet argmax cell after R. They are not equivalent for PoseNet or code length. G17 therefore treats the realized representative as a gauge variable, not as an arbitrary paint chosen once a debt cell flips. For every registered topology state, the scheduler retains distinct exact receiver-realizable representatives that are Seg-equivalent through R and compares their **joint** chronological A descendants by full score:

```text
Y1* in argmin over registered receiver-realizable Y1 with fixed Seg cell
    sqrt(10*d_pose(A(Y1),Y1)) + 25*B(P,G,Y1,A)/37_545_489,
with any changed Seg debt included as 100*d_seg in the actual comparison.
```

Seg equivalence is an exact frozen-scorer fact on received uint8 outputs, never a proxy margin or encoder label. The selected representative may trade texture, chroma, palette, event ordering, or shared-grammar spelling to make both A and outer coding cheaper while preserving the evaluator cell. No representative is selected from d_seg alone, and a locally cheapest G8 payload cannot suppress a more compressible or pose-legible descendant. The executable surface remains owed as `G17_EVALUATOR_EQUIVALENT_GAUGE_SELECTION_IMPLEMENTATION_OWED`; V1's branch manifest must at least preserve registered representative alternatives through complete G7 measurement rather than collapse them prematurely.

## 4. The counted G17 production domain

G17 reuses the canonical `TACPGA3` monolithic directory and its exact `P -> G -> A -> optional T` ordering. It does not fork ZIP construction. The fourth `TERMINAL_QUOTIENT` section becomes the production-domain seal; the frozen G10 receiver's `TERMINAL_QUOTIENT_CONSUMER_OWED` remains true for G10, while the new G17 receiver is the sole V1 consumer.

The frozen G10 concrete receiver is bounded to n2. Calling that receiver repeatedly is **not** an n600 production envelope: 600 pairs require 300 n2 fragments, so the exact counted 84,536-byte P would appear `300 * 84,536 = 25,360,800` raw bytes, of which `299 * 84,536 = 25,276,264` are duplicate P bytes. More fundamentally, 300 separate n2 archives violate the one-member population object and have no n600 receive/order proof. Even a hypothetical width-four repetition would carry `150 * 84,536 = 12,680,400` raw P bytes instead of one P. These fragment repetitions are non-candidates regardless of any shard-local score.

### 4.1 Population G/A section ABIs

The `TACG17G1` G-section packet has exact magic `TACG17G\0`; the `TACG17A1` A-section packet has exact magic `TACG17A\0`. Both use version 1, a fixed 88-byte big-endian header `>8sBBHHHII32s32s`, an explicit layout byte, fixed 50-byte descriptors `>HHBBII32sI`, a concatenated nested-payload body, and one big-endian `>I` footer. Header fields in order are:

```text

layout:u8                        # SHARDED=0; GLOBAL=1 is A-only
population_pair_start:u16
population_pair_count:u16
entry_count:u16
directory_bytes:u32              # exactly 50*entry_count
payload_bytes:u32
parent_binding_sha256[32]
payload_body_sha256[32]          # SHA256 of exact concatenated nested payloads
```

Each descriptor is:

```text

pair_count:u16
family:u8
mode:u8
payload_offset:u32               # relative to payload-body start
payload_bytes:u32
payload_sha256[32]
payload_crc32:u32
```

The footer is `zlib.crc32(header || directory || payload_body) & 0xffffffff`. Exact total length is `88 + 50*entry_count + payload_bytes + 4`. G accepts only `SHARDED=0`; A accepts exactly `SHARDED=0` or `GLOBAL=1`. The canonical shard partition is the unique consecutive partition of the declared population into width-four windows and one final width-one-to-four window. Thus n2 has one shard, n24 has six, and n600 has 150. In `SHARDED=0`, both G and A directories contain exactly one descriptor for every canonical shard, sorted by `pair_start`, with no gap, overlap, duplicate, or alternate partition. Descriptor payload offsets are contiguous from zero with no gaps. An active nested payload strict-parses, re-encodes identically, and agrees with its descriptor window/family/mode. A canonical PASS descriptor has zero payload bytes, SHA-256 of empty bytes, CRC32 zero, and consumes no body range. Zero-entry G/A sections are forbidden because they would erase population coverage. Unknown layout values, families/modes, foreign bindings, trailing bytes, or any CRC/hash drift fail closed.

For `TACG17G\0`, `parent_binding_sha256` is the exact P-section SHA-256. G-family wires are `PASS_PREDICTOR=1`, `SELECTIVE_ROW3=2`, and `EXACT_SEMANTIC_DIAGNOSTIC=3`; G-mode wires are `SEMANTIC_ONLY=0` and `SEMANTIC_THEN_FRESH_G8=1`. Canonical PASS/no-G8 is the required empty `(PASS_PREDICTOR, SEMANTIC_ONLY)` descriptor for that shard. The only active payloads admitted are:

- `(PASS_PREDICTOR, SEMANTIC_THEN_FRESH_G8)` -> exact `TACPG81` in `PASS_THEN_G8_V1` mode;
- `(SELECTIVE_ROW3, SEMANTIC_ONLY)` -> exact selected `TACG1C`;

For `TACG17A\0`, `parent_binding_sha256` is exactly `SHA256(b"G17-A-PARENT-V1\0" || p_section_sha256_bytes || g_section_sha256_bytes)`. A-family wires are `CANONICAL_PASS=0`, `NATIVE_PASS_CONDITIONAL=1`, `NATIVE_SELECTIVE_NO_G8=2`, `NATIVE_SELECTIVE_POST_G8=3`, `G13_PASS_SOURCE_XIP2=4`, and `G17_GENERAL_CONDITIONAL_XIP2=5`. Canonical sharded PASS A is an empty `(CANONICAL_PASS, PASS_P0)` descriptor. Explicit nested PASS packets are accepted only as measurement controls and canonicalize to that empty descriptor before final whole-object selection; this prevents byte-distinct no-op aliases. Shard descriptors may use different lawful active families, so pair-local inverse solutions are not suppressed by a single global A choice.

The only alternative to sharded A layout is **`GLOBAL=1` A layout**: exactly one descriptor covers the entire declared population, family is `G17_GENERAL_CONDITIONAL_XIP2`, mode is `GLOBAL_COPY_FINAL_Y1` or `QUANTIZED_XIP2`, and its sole payload is one exact `TACX2A4`. The explicit header layout, not entry count or nested packet shape, distinguishes global from sharded. This is binding at n2, where both layouts necessarily have one full-window descriptor and may lawfully carry byte-identical `TACX2A4` payloads: bounded/sharded intent encodes `layout=0`, population-global intent encodes `layout=1`, and no parser may infer or canonicalize one into the other.
