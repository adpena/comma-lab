G72 does **not** emit a `TSPPV1` packet. The current G49 analytic ABI is not the
V15 scorer-plane representation that the proposed experiment needs:

1. the semantic base decoder is the legacy
   `CarrierComposeReceiverV1.render_pairs.v9_v13` scorer-grid path, while the
   fresh V15 base is defined by `render_camera_pairs` followed by the exact
   integer half-up evaluator resize;
2. G49 erases `BoundaryShearletAtomV1.role` from the analytic operand; Road and
   Undrivable atoms with otherwise identical coordinates encode to identical
   bytes; and
3. G49 applies the resulting mask after RGB painting, while the V9/V15 donor
   atom is role-aware and acts before ordered role painting. The current
   spelling therefore loses occlusion semantics.

Packing scorer-native operands through that ABI would be a fake implementation,
even if parse-back passed. G72 instead lands the smallest real missing source
contract:

- recursive current-source custody for V15, G46, and G51;
- the real V9 Fisher/margin `BoundaryShearletAtomV1` proposal derivation with
  role preserved;
- five exact 120-pair immutable/atomic stage checkpoints; and
- exact whole-object joint admission with no independent segmentation, pose,
  or byte thresholds.

The effective competitive target remains the official displayed `0.172`; G72
moves it by zero.
