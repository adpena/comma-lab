## What the sources actually supply

The [nLab categorical-spectrum entry](https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/categorical+spectrum),
attributing the definition to Stefanich's 2021 thesis §§8.6 and 13, defines a categorical
spectrum as pointed higher categories `(C_n,X_n)_{n in N}` with
`C_n ~= End_{C_(n+1)}(X_(n+1))` and `X_n |-> id_(X_(n+1))`.

It also states that stagewise Picard infinity-groupoids give
`(-)^× : CatSpectra -> Spectra`. Kern's
[arXiv:2410.02578](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.02578), Theorem 3.1.7, identifies
categorical spectra with pointed `(infinity,Z)`-categories and identifies stable cells with
their natural cells. Masuda's [arXiv:2605.03114](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.03114)
develops their tensor product and categorical stability. These are structure/equivalence
results, not quantitative convergence estimates.

Stefanich's original thesis is hosted outside the authority's allowed network domains. Its
bibliographic location and attributed definition were checked through nLab and Kern; no
unread off-domain theorem is promoted here.

Benjamin–Lam–Luo [arXiv:2607.07786](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07786) gives typed
defect Hilbert spaces, lasso operators, fusion/associativity data, invertible and
non-invertible lines, and a direct-sum case where only operators acting on the same defect
space compose. It gives no visual-codec codelength law or rate allocator.

## 3. Picard shadow: one finite quotient-compatibility criterion

**Verdict scope:** the current common-affine description gauge and named inter-level map, not
Picard methods or gauge quotients as families.

The Picard functor retains invertible objects and morphisms. That makes it an organizer for
already-defined invertible gauges; it does not discover which transformations are scorer-null.

Here is the complete finite/1-categorical statement. At ladder level `n`, let `G_n` be the
group of explicitly implemented invertible description changes, let `H_n normal G_n` be the
subgroup declared gauge-redundant, and let `phi_n : G_(n+1) -> G_n` be the implemented
inter-level homomorphism. Then

```text
[g] |-> [phi_n(g)] : G_(n+1)/H_(n+1) -> G_n/H_n
```

is well-defined **iff** `phi_n(H_(n+1))` is contained in `H_n`. For finite tables this is
decidable by enumerating closure, identity, inverses, normality, homomorphism preservation,
and that inclusion. This is **DERIVED elementary group theory**; categorical spectra add the
stagewise Picard packaging, not the criterion.

The affine description form is even cheaper. Let `V_n` be a coefficient space,
`A_n : K^(d+1) -> V_n` inject the common-affine gauge, and `F_n : V_(n+1) -> V_n` be the
actual inter-level decode/translation map. For a general `F_n`, quotient descent requires
`F_n(v+h)-F_n(v)` to lie in `im A_n` for every `v` and every `h` in `im A_(n+1)`. If `F_n`
is explicitly verified affine, `F_n(v)=L_n v+c_n`, the criterion reduces exactly to

```text
L_n(im A_(n+1)) subset im A_n,
```

equivalently, in finite linear coordinates, when some `B_n` satisfies
`L_n A_(n+1) = A_n B_n`. Rank `A_n=d+1` certifies that exactly `d+1` continuous directions
are removed at that level. The matrix factorization is not asserted for a nonlinear decoder.

**Current Pact instantiation:** adding the same `u^T z+v` to all five affine class scores in
the measured `d=4` head leaves pairwise score differences unchanged, so the head-description
quotient removes `d+1=5` directions and yields the already-derived 20-scalar/138-byte
margin-preserving construction
([Nielsen crosswalk](nielsen_infogeo_crosswalk_20260719_codex.md)). It does **not** prove
receiver float32 equality, a spatial/RGB inverse, or scorer equivalence after `R`.

**Named consumers:** Task #539, `src/tac/boundary_math/power_diagram_witness.py`, and the
existing `src/tac/witness_dsl/gauge.py` meta-layer. **$0 gate:** materialize the two adjacent
description actions and `F_n`; verify the finite table or matrix condition above; then require
decode/re-encode identity, exact pairwise-logit differences, and the frame-195 near-tie fixture
