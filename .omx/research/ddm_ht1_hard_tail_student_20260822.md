## Conclusion

Do not ship or train a second, separately priced hard-tail student on the present evidence. Fold one hard-tail teacher ablation into the NR1 implicit task-cell quotient, then let a matched held-out and receiver-closed A/B decide whether it survives. The explicit 25-row tail representation is closed on the current instance: every row received zero bytes in the later own-scorer-mass allocator, and every DM4 realized row costs more than its own measured byte cap. That closes this formulation, not the teacher information, the implicit-student family, or the task-cell paradigm.

The teacher signal is still live. The 25 exact semantic records cost only 4,124 B independently and 1,569 B jointly. A deterministic bucket-family held-out persistence test favored history for 32/36 cells overall and for 21/25 bucket-family analogues of the DM rows. A sibling realization then reduced the naive RGB realization from 3,960,549 B to 3,241,321 B, a 719,228 B or 18.1598% reduction. Those facts show compressible recurrence and improvable realization, but neither is a public receiver-closed student score.

The minimum admissible student is therefore not a new archive section. It is a from-birth training condition inside NR1's single implicit quotient. It may consume C1/DM teacher examples while fitting, but it earns shipped bytes only if the final quotient itself lowers complete receiver-closed score. After the frozen r9 endpoint and NR1 body exist, the residual tail must be recomputed; any event already represented by the quotient receives neither a duplicate symbol nor a duplicate byte allowance.

## Minimal fold-in student

The only student worth an authorized test is a matched NR1 ablation with these fixed boundaries:

1. **One body, no tail section.** Use NR1's single implicit task-cell quotient and existing public receiver. Do not introduce a second decoder, event packet, row table, site list, or learned sidecar.
2. **Endpoint teacher is primary.** The frozen JO r9 endpoint defines the target. C1/DM examples only emphasize residual endpoint failures; correct endpoint cells and nearby pixels are explicit collateral negatives.
3. **Fit through the public action.** Train from birth through `R -> uint8 -> frozen SegNet/PoseNet`, with quantization/parse-back compensation and a fresh terminal dxi. Semantic record reconstruction alone is not an objective verdict.
4. **Prevent leakage in the engineering gate.** Group all DM records from the same `pair_id`; hold out entire pair groups, stratified by bucket where possible. No held-out pair, derived event, or static analogue may enter fitting. Report per-fold conditional bytes, realized Seg/Pose action, and repeated-run noise separately.
5. **Recompute after the body exists.** At harvest, subtract all C1/DM events already solved by the endpoint-only NR1 body. Duplicate coverage receives zero symbols and zero marginal byte credit.
6. **Dynamic complete-score budget.** Let `B_tail <= B_max(D_student) - B_all_other_actual`. There is no reserved 33.5 KB, 1,569 B, or 42,382 B allowance. The 42,382 B figure is the whole-archive cut required at DX2 distortion, not a tail budget.
7. **Admission is end-to-end.** The hard-tail ablation survives only if its receiver-closed complete archive has `ΔS_complete < 0` against the matched endpoint-only NR1 control and remains inside the then-current sub-0.12 byte cap. A semantic, proxy, component-only, or non-public win is insufficient.

This is deliberately a training ablation rather than an implementation landing. Building a separate HT1 payload now would repeat a measured losing formulation and would violate the no-fake boundary by presenting teacher description length as receiver-closed value.

- **Connection/history student subform: instance-scoped mixed result.** Four of 25 bucket-family analogues were negative; 21 were positive. DM3 did not test the original rows or an evaluator-native student.
- **Naive generic RGB realization: formulation closed.** Its `2524.250478x` exchange ratio is dominated, but DM4's 18.1598% sibling improvement proves the number is not intrinsic to the semantic target.
- **DM4-style separately encoded L3 tail: formulation closed on all 25 current rows.** LP1/MS7 later priced every row at zero under its own guaranteed scorer reach.
- **One-bit signed bounded-uint8 C1 packet: formulation closed.** Joint realization loses after rate despite high singleton feasibility.
- **Implicit shared task-cell quotient: open.** No retained receipt has run the matched held-out/public-receiver ablation described above.
- **Task-cell paradigm: open.** No result here justifies a paradigm or family kill.
