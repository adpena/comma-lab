## Conclusion

PR135 is not a new codec family. It is PR130's semantic-token/HPAC/CPR1 vehicle, with PR133's constrained-basis and re-solved int12 carrier, followed by five kinds of work:

1. fixed-schema lossless packing of the learned state;
2. WANS1 packing of the unchanged renderer tensors;
3. CAP1 predictive/Rice packing of the selected carrier;
4. RC64 coding of the semantic-token stream;
5. exact joint renderer/carrier optimization: two W4 FiLM edits, a five-event frame-0 selector, and a full int12 carrier re-solve.

The new competitive lesson is not "find another general-purpose compressor." Against PR135's source-reported unrounded score, our exact lc2 row is worse by `0.007330573992722689 S`. Only `0.00033426119446733 S` of that is the rate contribution implied by the externally reported 502-byte archive difference. The PoseNet root term accounts for `0.006973665376925137 S`, about 95% of the gap. PR135 won mainly by jointly re-solving frame 0 after changing the frame-1 renderer, while also banking a collection of small lossless wins.

This intake did not move our pointer. The official leaderboard displays PR135 at `0.162`; the PR author reports the unrounded value `0.16226842169958583`. The maintainer said "added to leaderboard," but there is no public evaluator-bot component receipt in the PR conversation. Until the exact archive is under our custody and replayed, the unrounded components remain **external source-reported**, not our authority row.

## LIVE-HYPOTHESES

- A same-state lc2-ANS port to F26 can recover part of the final ~404-byte deficit to displayed 0.162 because lc2's token payload is 178 bytes smaller than F26's reported RC64 payload. It remains plausible but weak because the streams use different probabilities, residuals, and containers.
- WANS1 plus lc2's split/CX2 transforms may have favorable whole-archive interaction on unchanged PR130/F26 state because both exploit fixed field structure at different levels. Only a complete ZIP race can establish the sign.
- Joint FiLM/carrier coordinate descent should transfer as a method to #995/#1007 because PR135's accepted sequence improved all three score terms and converged to zero accepted singleton moves; the learned values themselves do not transfer.
- Native RC64 may close lc2's contest-CPU timeout even if it loses a few hundred rate bytes because F26's reported token decode time is an order of magnitude below lc2's measured CPU token wall. A same-host, same-state runtime race is required.
