## Authority and grading method

The ladder used here is `INSTANCE < FORMULATION < FAMILY < PARADIGM`; a result stays at the narrowest
rung its evidence supports. Frozen replay, a borrowed theorem, a disengaged loss, an `n<600` probe, a
constructed proxy, or non-authority hardware is never silently promoted. Small magnitude alone is not
a dismissal: every retained negative below also has either a preregistered threshold, an exact terminal
solve/certificate, a semantic or identification impossibility, or a missing-authority refusal. Every
negative carves a future design constraint; a cure's existence is not evidence that the cure works.

`Cure adjacency` below distinguishes `BUILT-never-fired`, `needs-build`, `reformulation-queue`, and
logging-only apparatus. An adjacent artifact violates a negative only when it carries a matched measured
fact on the same authority surface. None does after scope correction.

## Interaction findings

### I1 — N1 + N2 + N3: oracle-versus-extractability gap

The same-area exact oracle retains `52.7815%` support mass, yet three rounds of shallow cheap-feature
fitting top out at approximately `20.17%`, while direction cosines remain near zero. Exact terminal
solves remove “train longer” and optimizer failure as explanations. The surviving bottleneck is
**representation/identifiability**: useful costate support exists, but it is not exposed in the shallow
first-pre-SE chart. The next experiment must change the information surface—deeper/global/nonlinear,
dense-label, or on-policy—not ridge, RFF width, calibration, seed, or another convex selector.

### I2 — N1/N2/N3 + N7: feature/precision frontier

Int8 can slightly improve receiver-parsed aggregate Seg at the same checkpoint while shallow
feature compression catastrophically loses direction/support. Jointly these say precision is not the
current information bottleneck. Spend model/teacher complexity on **which global nonlinear features are
preserved**, not on preserving every FP32 degree of freedom. Quantization may be a receiver-aware
regularizer, but this remains instance-only until Pose, bytes, runtime, and score are measured; do not
confuse “int8-better here” with “lower precision is universally better.”

### I3 — N4 + N5: logging and experiment design dominate estimator choice

FORE and HCM fail for the same upstream reason at different plates: absent transitions/actions/support
and absent matched run-level treatments. No more sophisticated estimator can recover an unobserved
counterfactual. The causal manifest is the shared cure apparatus, but only prospectively: positive
propensity, execution, outcomes, coverage, and multiple trajectories/runs still have to be generated.

### I4 — N6 + N10: wrong-regime proxy pair

N6 observes a loss before it is active; N10 observes constructed proxy dynamics before the real
governed finishing boundary. Together they establish a general admission rule for gradient/optimizer
architecture: **the treatment and all compared forces must be engaged under the production transition
law before a conflict or improvement verdict exists**. Pre-engagement probes are useful for probe design,
not retirement.

### I5 — N9 + N10: geometry has built adjacent treatments

The S2 Fisher-head proposal and raw-matrix MuonH proposal both have narrower already-built cures:
`HeadGeometry` and the FiLM polar-chart SPEL finisher. Neither has fired. Catalog #307 therefore routes
work toward implementation measurement, not a new geometry paradigm or a magnitude-based dismissal.

### I6 — N6 + N8 + N12: launch observability is the evidence cure

N6 needs exact screw/phase engagement and N8 refuses a proxy plateau clock. N12's D-B engagement hook
and D-A component accounting are therefore not ancillary telemetry: they are the apparatus that turns
the next governed trajectory into admissible evidence. Removing those gates would recreate both
negative-result errors.

### I7 — N7 + N10: composition order remains an open interaction

The exact int8 improvement was measured on the current parsed representation; the FiLM polar treatment
changes the folded matrix/gain chart. Quantization after retraction/fold may alter both error and rate.
Neither memo measures this composition, so the future polar-chart A/B must report pre/post-fold
quantization survival rather than importing N7's sign.
