## Domain 1 — Neural SDF / INR representation SOTA since 2024

**The headline find of the whole sweep: arXiv 2506.11139 "Grids Often Outperform Implicit Neural Representations at Compressing Dense Signals" (Kim & Fridovich-Keil, NeurIPS 2025).** Systematic INR-vs-grid study (2D/3D, real+synthetic, varying effective bandwidth, overfitting AND inverse tasks): a simple **regularized grid + interpolation trains faster and to equal/higher quality than ANY INR at matched parameter count for dense signals** — with a named exception: **"fitting binary signals such as shape contours" is where INRs win**. Deep-math reading: a grid is the identity basis — optimal when the signal has full-support spectral mass (dense texture); an INR's advantage is exactly the **measure-zero codim-1 structure** where a global nonlinear chart amortizes the contour (our Laguerre-separatrix / cartoon-class object, N-term O(N⁻²) vs grid O(N⁻¹)). This is a leaderboard-grade, adversarially-reviewed **published validation of the witness vehicle choice**: our target IS the binary-contour regime (piecewise-constant argmax partition), and our measured story (task-space INR at low rate where bc20-style small dense carriers under-perform) is the same phase boundary seen from the other side.
- **DRAW-FROM (probe P1, $0):** hybrid split along their phase boundary — regularized-grid arm for the SMOOTH bulk classes (Road/Undrivable/hood interiors, where argmax is deep-margin and grid bits are cheap+trainable-fast) + coord-INR reserved for the boundary annulus/lane manifold. We already capacity-route WITHIN the INR; this asks whether the bulk should leave the INR entirely. A/B at matched counted-bytes, through-R, n600. Also their regularization recipe for grids = a calibrated baseline arm we've never run at optimal form.
- **Contradiction ledger:** none with our measurements — it REFINES them. But it kills any residual "INR is universally better at low rate" prior; cite it whenever a dense-signal sub-carrier (texture side-channel, chroma bulk) is proposed as an INR by reflex.

**FINER++ (arXiv 2407.19434; FINER CVPR'24 2312.02434, code github.com/liuzhen0212/FINER):** variable-periodic activation FAMILY (Sine/Gauss/**Wavelet** backbones) — bias-init range SELECTS which sub-frequency of the variable-periodic function activates → the supported frequency set becomes tunable per-neuron. Direct hit on our UNSWEPT `gauss`/step activation lever (CLAUDE.md frontier §lever 5) and the measured fixed-β hosc divergence (tanh saturation): FINER++'s mechanism is an alternative to β-annealing — instead of annealing a global sharpness, the bias-init distributes per-neuron frequency selection, sidestepping the saturation → vanishing-grad death we measured.

## Domain 3 — Tropical geometry / Maslov post-#284

**Someone DOES compress via the tropical skeleton — but networks, not data: TropNNC (arXiv 2409.03945, v3 Dec 2025).** Structured (neuron-level) compression of ReLU nets: output = tropical rational function; compression = reduction of the tropical polynomials (geometrically: **zonotope/Newton-polytope reduction**, Hoffman-style approximation); claims the **tightest known theoretical compression bound**, works **data-free** (weights only), matches ThiNet/CUP on ImageNet `[external]`, first tropical treatment of conv layers. Sister with an exact algorithm: **2306.15157 "Revisiting Tropical Polynomial Division"** (Maragos lineage) — tropical division ⇔ convex hulls of unions of polyhedra, exact NN-simplification algorithm. Also: Brandenburg et al., "The Real Tropical Geometry of Neural Networks for Binary Classification" (OpenReview I7JWf8XA2w) — the decision-boundary-as-real-tropical-variety formalism for the trained (not just architectural) regime; 2602.03204 (MoE expressivity via tropical combinatorial depth); tropical best-approximation solvers (2507.22634, 2403.16337 — max-plus rational fitting with finite-step convergence = principled piecewise-LINEAR fitting machinery).
- **Why this matters to US specifically:** our COUNTED bytes ARE network weights (the witness head). #284 proved the witness's τ→0 limit is a tropical object (Maslov). TropNNC says: once trained, the tropical skeleton of that object admits a principled, bound-carrying reduction — i.e., a **rate lever on the counted witness weights that respects exactly the geometry (Laguerre cells) that d_seg lives on**, unlike magnitude pruning which is geometry-blind. The Newton-polytope reduction deletes monomials whose tropical hypersurface contributions never win the max — in our language, deletes parameters whose cells never own an argmax region in [0,1]².
- **HONEST LIMITS:** (a) TropNNC is exact for ReLU/linear; our witness head is smooth (sine/step/softmax-of-SDF) — the tropicalization only bites in the τ→0 / hard-argmax limit, so the reduction must be applied to the FINAL frozen witness and verified through-R (the argmax is what ships, so the smooth-vs-ReLU gap may be immaterial AT the partition — that is a measurable claim); (b) ancestor discipline — their ImageNet numbers transfer nothing; (c) capacity-cliff memory (PR95 prune = cliff) — but that was RGB-reconstruction pruning of a borrowed decoder; a partition-preserving reduction is a different object. Code availability unverified — check before build.
- **DRAW-FROM (probe P5, $0, post-#205):** on a FROZEN trained witness, apply Laguerre-cell-aware reduction (drop parameters whose removal provably/measurably leaves the n600 argmax partition unchanged through-R — the tropical-division test, approximated by per-parameter partition-diff), report Δcounted-bytes at Δd_seg=0. This is "compress via the tropical skeleton" run at OUR vehicle. Modest expected ΔS (rate is not the wall) — but it is the cleanest post-training free rate win with a geometric guarantee, and the first published-math match for the #284 tropical layer.
