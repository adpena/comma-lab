## 2. Deconstruction

### PR130 semantic-pose-HPAC_CPR1 (priority) — what HPAC is, what carries what

**HPAC** = Hierarchical Parallel Autoregressive ConvNet (Li et al. 2025, lossless image coding):
patch-group causal factorization + Type-A 7×7 / depthwise Type-B (5×5 dil-2, 3×3 dil-4) masked
convs + prev-frame token context (`conv_past`) + per-frame FiLM. Lineage is **PR#86 (jas0xf
semantic-token/HPAC) — NOT HNeRV-family**; `LINEAGE_AND_CITATIONS.md` is an exemplary
borrowed-substrate accounting (mechanism-by-mechanism inherited/original table).

- **Seg carrier = the partition itself.** A 5-class semantic TOKEN STREAM for the 600 scored
  frames at 384×512 is stored losslessly, arithmetic-coded (constriction) under the HPAC learned
  prior: **semantic stream 40,252B** + **models (renderer + HPAC, int4/int8 packed, xz) 73,944B**.
  A small learned renderer (width-96, coordinate channels, 4 dilated residual blocks, frame
  embeddings → RGB head) renders frame_1 FROM the tokens so SegNet re-derives (approximately)
  the stored partition → d_seg 2.966e-4. "Store the scored quantity" realized at scale.
- **Pose carrier = standalone neutral-gray low-rank frame_0.** `slave = round(clamp(127.5 +
  64·normalize(einsum(coeff[600×12], basis[12×24×32]))))` — 12-bit delta-zigzag coeffs +
  quantized learned basis, **23,054B**, canonical-Huffman + Rice repacked (CPR1 lossless repack
  −3,328B). frame_0 carries ZERO semantic content; it is a pure PoseNet actuator. d_pose
  2.331e-5. This exploits exactly the frozen-scorer factorization we measured independently
  (SegNet reads last frame only → frame_0 is seg-free).
- **Determinism:** integer-lattice HPAC inference (bounded integer intermediates, dyadic
  requantization, canonical entropy logits on a 1/8-logit lattice) → symbol/token-exact decode
  across GPU architectures; RGB bit-identity honestly NOT claimed; portability gate = bitstream
  decodes + official evaluator succeeds.
- LOC ≈ 2,982 (submission dir). Training pipeline (multi-day model fitting) NOT in the PR.

### PR129 qlp_exactgrid — what "exactgrid" is

Latents-only polish of the frozen PR95/101 HNeRV decoder + PR112 ctx range coder (banned-lineage
vehicle for us; lessons-only). **"Exact grid" = the straight-through latent quantizer replicates
the shipping container's packing lattice bit-for-bit** (per-dim fp16 min/scale, uint8 codes), so
the optimizer's forward IS the shipped decode — zero train/pack gap. Seg loss = smooth argmax-flip
fraction `sigmoid(−margin/τ)`, τ annealed 0.20→0.07 (boundary-pixel concentration). Plus FS1B:
re-searched per-pair frame_0 pixel-mode pose selector (35 modes, +48B, seg-neutral by the same
frame_0/frame_1 factorization). Trained entirely on Apple MPS against frozen scorers; exact-eval
verified on CPU. It does NOT solve against the scorer grid in a lattice/preimage sense — it is
gradient descent WITH the storage lattice in-loop (realization-aware descent, not an inverse
solve). Convergence with our laws, not with our M2-preimage line.

**The reframe (writeup §1):** *"the true entropy isn't the video itself, but the expected outputs
of the evaluation models... The answer itself is the true entropy."* Independent public discovery
of our evaluator-equivalent-witness paradigm. FROZEN-SPACE insight (property of the scoring
function, not of any vehicle).

**Adversarial existence proof (writeup §2, Table 1):** direct gradient descent on INPUT PIXELS
against the frozen evaluators (Adam + Huber, C&W margin, per-dim normalized PoseNet outputs):
**SegNet 100% agreement (0.00 seg) + PoseNet MSE <1e-9 in 2h on one T4** (99.99% / <1e-6 in
30min). To humans the frames are static garbage. FROZEN-SPACE reachability fact: near-zero
distortion points exist and are cheap to FIND at unconstrained rate; the whole game is rate.
(They abandoned it because storing per-pixel adversarial frames busts rate and inflate-time
generation would need scorers in-archive — same compliance wall we codified.)

**Architecture (inflate.py + writeup §7):**
- **Token alphabet = the 5 SegNet classes**; token grid = 384×512 (SegNet's own input grid, NOT
  camera res) × 600 master frames. The stored object IS the (near-)argmax partition.
- **Master renderer `TokenRendererV62`** (~31KB gz): one-hot(5) → 3-layer 3×3 stride-1 CNN
  (32ch, GroupNorm, GELU) + per-pair FiLM (600×8 embedding → 64) → sigmoid×255 → bilinear up to
  874×1164. **Receptive field EXACTLY 7 by derivation:** writeup §7.2 localizes SegNet's boundary
  decision to the stem (stride-2 3×3) + Stage-0 depthwise 3×3 ⇒ 7-input-pixel window; 3 layers
  is the unique depth matching it (RF 5 under-covers, RF 9 wastes capacity the metric can't see).
  Cross-GPU FiLM table baked on CPU FP32 (cuBLAS tiny-matmul kernels differ Ada vs Turing).
- **Slave renderer `ShrinkSingleNeRV`** (~32KB gz): 6-float per-pair latent → Linear stem →
  5× (depthwise 3×3 + 1×1 + PixelShuffle) → per-pair RGB bias; LSQ+ INT4 throughout; NO FiLM,
  NO skips (deliberate: latent already carries frame identity). Pose duty only; SegNet never
  sees it. Latents total 1.8KB.
- **HPAC token coder** (27.6KB PPMd'd model + 113.9KB tokens.bin): PixelCNN-class masked-conv AR
  prior made 30-min-feasible. Two-level partition: 12×16=192 independent P=32 patches (conditioned
  cross-frame via prev-frame tokens `conv_past` + CausalSPM cross-patch summary) × **group order**
  s = c + δ·r (δ=2) inside each patch ⇒ **94 sequential group steps/frame instead of 196,608
  raster steps (~2000× fewer)**; T4 end-to-end decode ~18 min. Type-A/B masks live in the conv
  weights (zero out δ·dr+dc ≥ 0 / > 0). Arithmetic coder = constriction; bpp 0.00773 (1067× vs
  raw token stream; 3.1× better than bz2; within 1.3× of their measured no-quant entropy floor).
- **SCN (Self-Compressing Networks)** for master + HPAC weights: learnable per-layer bit-budget
  IN the loss — the optimizer allocates quantization precision; +PPMd over quantized state-dict
  bytes ≈ 2× further. Ship split: master 31,154 + slave 32,210 + meta 1,563 + hpac 27,579 +
  tokens 113,860 = 207,579B.
- **Failed variant worth remembering:** HPAC "residual prediction direction" — factorizing as
  p(class-diff | prev frame) instead of p(class | spatial ctx, prev frame) — **3.5× WORSE bpp
  (0.02786 vs 0.00797), abandoned** (writeup Table 7). VEHICLE-SCOPED number; directional lesson
  for temporal grammars: condition on the past, don't code the label-diff.
- **GT-decode custody incident (writeup §8.1):** pre-DALI phases trained against PyAV-decoded GT;
  train-set d_seg 0.000035 vs evaluator 0.000712 (**20× gap** from NVDEC vs PyAV chroma/color
  conversion); shipped checkpoint required a DALI-corrected fine-tune. Their independent instance
  of our GT-decode custody law.

**qpose #67/#79 skim (merged `qpose14_*` trees):** decode-time `make_dct_basis()` GENERATES the
DCT basis deterministically in inflate (zero rate; only per-pair coeffs + scale stored;
`basis_kind ∈ {dct, poly_fourier}` for smooth per-pair pose trajectories). Prior art for low-rank
pose actuation AND an in-the-wild instance of our "compile the generator into inflate.py, count
only the video-derived payload" doctrine. PR130's contribution over qpose = LEARNED basis +
neutral-gray standalone carrier (their lineage doc is candid about this boundary).

   (CPU-baked FiLM table; PR130's integer-lattice entropy path) — both laws we hold; both
   independently re-discovered.

**Divergences (where OUR line differs and why the gap is our opportunity):**
6. *RF-7 boundary localization vs our separatrix geometry.* Their sharpest architectural claim:
   SegNet's class boundaries are decided by the stem+Stage-0 **7-input-pixel window**; deeper
   stages only do region classification; a renderer with RF exactly 7 suffices to place
   boundaries (working existence proof at d_seg 6.8e-4; PR130's dilated variant reached 2.97e-4).
   Our measured picture (SegNet-sees-REGIONS, head rank-4 flip distance, ERF r50~85px) is about
   the FULL flip mechanism, theirs about edge LOCALIZATION — complementary, not contradictory,
   but their claim implies **boundary-placement capacity is cheap (3 conv layers)**; the
   expensive part is knowing WHERE (the tokens). That is exactly our describe-line thesis: the
   information is the partition geometry, not the renderer. Treat RF-7 as a FROZEN-SPACE
   candidate fact to verify on our own apparatus before consuming (their derivation + existence
   proof, not our measurement).
7. *Generic AR prior vs structural description (THE Vehicle-B benchmark).* Their seg leg prices
   the partition with a LEARNED GENERIC prior: PR86 ≈ 31K renderer + 27.6K prior + 113.9K tokens
   ≈ 172KB → d_seg 6.8e-4; PR130 ≈ 74K models + 40.3K tokens ≈ 114KB → d_seg 2.97e-4. Neither
   exploits scene structure (BEV staticity, ego-screw, lane geometry, worldsheet events) beyond
   prev-frame conditioning + patch locality. **Card §12 Vehicle B's job restated in their
   currency: beat ~114KB-for-the-partition by re-describing it in a vocabulary where the scene's
   actual generative structure (what our DDM grammar encodes) makes the stream short.** Their
   failed diff-factorization (lesson §2b) warns HOW not to do the temporal part.
8. *Per-frame memorization vs global structure.* Their FiLM-per-pair embeddings + per-pair
   latents/coeffs memorize frames individually. Our line compresses the RUN (one BEV scene + one
   ξ-curve). Their operating point leaves this entire axis unexploited — consistent with our
   thesis that the remaining rate headroom lives in cross-frame structure.

**What 0.1721 @ 191KB (seg 2.97e-4, pose 2.33e-5) implies for card §12:** the seam composition
