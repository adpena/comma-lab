## Gate behavior

Pose population checks snapshot the pointer before measurement and read d_pose from its promoted source receipt. The receipt's SHA must match both the mirror and the pointer's own pin; archive, runtime digest, n600 and passed status must agree. Pointer changes during measurement refuse. The measured mean must lie in [1/3,3] of the reference before an NPY or report can be written as base. `--pose-base-differs-because` requires a concrete rationale and yields an explicit waiver receipt. Nonfinite/negative values cannot be excused by that flag.

Pair-level fe1/rw1 measurements use `--base-pose-reference`, a finite nonnegative n600 NPY vector. Both its population mean and the supplied population scalar are checked against the pointer; measured pairs are compared with their matching reference pairs. Comparing an individual hard pair with the population mean would create false alarms. Reference bytes are hashed and read once. Existing archive/carrier identity checks remain: a threefold magnitude test cannot prove the exact render or detect every wrong subset.

The coder gate resolves the pointer's runtime through its promoted receipt and fire manifest, checks its archive and pinned runtime custody, then hashes both source trees with **the same** `tac.candidate_seal.measure_runtime_digest` function.
