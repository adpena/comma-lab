## Cross-vehicle evaluator facts

The scorer imposes an obligation matrix, not ordinary video fidelity:

```text
frame0: Pose only
frame1: Pose + Seg
archive.zip: exact counted rate
```

SegNet consumes only frame1 and scores hard argmax at 384x512. PoseNet consumes both frames after
bilinear resize and a 12-channel two-frame YUV6 transform. Exact score is

```text
100*d_seg + sqrt(10*d_pose) + 25*bytes/37,545,489.
```

One Seg cell is worth `8.4771050347e-7`; one archive byte costs `6.6585895312e-7`; one cell buys
1.273108 bytes absent Pose. Pose has no constant linear price because its square-root marginal
depends on the current `d_pose`.

At the local pointer, reaching below 0.19 is arithmetically equivalent to 1,651.74 archive bytes or
1,297.41 net Seg cells if all other terms are held fixed. Reaching 0.15 is equivalent to 61,724.52
bytes or 48,483.33 cells under the same deliberately one-axis assumption. These are waterlines, not
predictions; Pose and component interactions must be recomputed at the realized candidate.

Three consequences are common to the whole line:

1. **Do not render symmetric obligations.** Frame0 should carry only the cheapest Pose-sufficient
   representation. Partition and texture payload on frame0 need an independently measured Pose
   benefit; they cannot earn Seg improvement.
2. **Do not substitute description length for a receiver section.** Every admitted component needs
   exact bytes, headers/quantizers, fresh-process decode, consumed output, R survival, and an archive
   hash. In-memory analytic GT constructions, ignored banks, and compressed temporary arrays are
   screening evidence only.
3. **Do not add local scorer gains as if independent.** Frozen GT boundaries are sparse, while
   SegNet has source-level global dependency paths. A recovered summary reports global margin-
   gradient tails, but its raw receipt is absent. Reproduce those tails and require decoded
   composite interaction checks, per-class guardrails, topology/turnover, and remote-region blocks.
4. **Exploit the exact resize kernel only through a compliant receiver.** The shared resize reads
   786,432 of 1,017,336 camera pixels and has disjoint 2x2 footprints; 230,904 camera pixels per frame
   are exact joint-preprocess blind coordinates. Fill them generically and solve sampled-footprint
   preimages blockwise, while still emitting exactly 3,662,409,600 raw bytes / 1,200 frames. A short
   raw is evaluator truncation and a NO-FAKE failure, never a rate lever.

The exact local Pose-preprocess kernel supplies the clean v7.5.3 replacement family: luma-null at
each scorer-grid pixel and zero-sum R/B over each 2x2 block. The six Haar/chroma atoms span that
linear kernel, but placement, sigmoid, camera-grid lift, uint8, resize, and clamp can all break it.
Only exact raw reload plus first-six Pose-output stability closes the claim.

For v8, the frozen RAG has 6,703 triple-or-higher 2x2 junctions. This makes cycle consistency an
actual decode constraint. Pairwise fields must be antisymmetric and integrable after quantization,
or the receiver must solve a globally defined labeling/potential problem. Sparse boundaries justify
where to spend bits; they do not prove pairwise scorer factorization.
