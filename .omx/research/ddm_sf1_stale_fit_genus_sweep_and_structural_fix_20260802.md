## THE GENUS

Any SOLVED or FITTED quantity has a PARTNER it was solved against — a base
archive, a vehicle, a checkpoint, a grid, an upstream scorer version, a
co-coordinate. When the partner MOVES the solved value is STALE. Nothing in the
artifact records the partner, so the staleness is INVISIBLE and recoverable only
by hand-archaeology — which is how all three known instances were found, one at
a time, after they had already shipped.
