## What it says (verbatim-grounded)
Diagnostic of deep-net MEMORIZATION under LABEL NOISE. Analyze the **centered scatter of per-example last-layer gradients** (a Fisher-type matrix); track its **effective rank** during training. Corrupted labels **inflate effective rank** by injecting spectral mass into low-energy/unused eigendirections (raises gradient-spectrum entropy). A **first-order leave-one-out attribution formula** quantifies each example's contribution to rank inflation + identifies when corrupted examples contribute more than clean ones. Characteristic **inflation→collapse trajectory**: rank expands transiently during memorization, contracts once corrupted labels are fully fit. Results: top-100 rank-contributors are 69.2–96.2% noisy (94.4% on CIFAR-10N); effective rank 28.88 (clean) → 97.09 (60% corruption); CIFAR-10/100/10N; SmallCNN/ResNet18/ViT.
