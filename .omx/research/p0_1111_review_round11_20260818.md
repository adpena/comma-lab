## Assumption-challenge axis (mandatory)

receipt is a durable property of the bytes.* It is not. It is a joint measurement
of the bytes, the instrument, and the world — and today both the instrument and
the world moved under a receipt that did not. Every round from 5 onward has
tightened freshness with respect to **scanned surfaces** while leaving the other
two inputs unguarded. The assumption is **CARGO-CULTED**, inherited from an era
when the checker and the frontier were both static.

The structural consequence, if MAIN wants a class cure rather than a fifth
instance fix: a receipt should record the **checker's own source sha** and the
**canonical-pointer state** it was measured against, and the freshness law should
invalidate on any of the three moving — surfaces, instrument, world. That would
have caught F1 automatically and would make "82/86" self-dating.
