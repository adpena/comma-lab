## IS F26/HPAC THE WRONG BASIS?

**For local entropy coding, no. For the sub-0.12 search space, yes.**

The distinction matters:

1. F26/HPAC is close to its local optimum for the exact shipped token field. Current-body
   memoryless coding, richer contexts, table corrections, model width, and packaging have been
   exhausted to bytes or tens of bytes. fx5 itself bought only 70 B.
2. On the earlier same-lineage decomposition, **110,862.39 B / 98.89%** of the token code was the
   binary question “does the exact token equal HPAC's argmax?” and only **1,247.19 B / 1.11%** was
   class refinement. Those are **MEASURED `[macOS-CPU scorer-free]` on the hv1 token field**, not
   transferred byte claims for fx5. They identify the structural bottleneck: surprise against one
   exact field, not class naming.
3. A masked copy/residual syntax by itself is not novel here. The hit/miss arithmetic stream already
   implements that idea, and richer fixed-field context ladders saturated. Recent conditional-
   residual literature therefore supplies a useful analogy, not an untried mechanism.
4. The representation has to stop requiring lossless reproduction of one token field. The lawful
   quotient is the set of learned fields whose decoded RGB stays in the same or a better frozen
   Seg/Pose evaluator cell. Searching that quotient changes `z`, the renderer, and the entropy model
   together. r012 deliberately did not test that family.

The proposed objective is the complete-container problem, not a proxy credit:

`min_(theta,z) 100*d_seg(theta,z) + sqrt(10*d_pose(theta,z)) + 25*ZIP_bytes(theta,z)/37,545,489`

subject to deterministic parse-back, full n600 stage boundaries, pose non-increase where JO1
requires it, retained per-candidate payloads, and receiver-time compliance. Generic decoder code may
be free; all learned or video-derived `theta,z` remain counted. An oracle label-space result is only
a lower bound and cannot be called a candidate.

## THE CELL-QUOTIENT SANDWICH

The one representation family that survives this synthesis is a three-layer sandwich:

1. **Warm semantic base:** inherit the conditioned shipping trunk; narrow or reorganize it without a
   fresh birth. This is the only width form not covered by WD3's failed fresh D56/F64 instances.
2. **Task-cell event field:** instead of transmitting every exact semantic token, transmit a compact
   causal program of deviations from a generic/warm predictor. The program may use group runs,
   connected boundary events, or learned prototype indices, but its field is allowed to change.
3. **JO1 joint closure:** select the base and events using the exact full-field `F/H/W`, Pose6, real
   coder bytes, parse-back, and deterministic-repeat gates. The correction is not a post-hoc gate;
   collateral and pose are in the solve.

This is a **HYPOTHESIS**, not an implementation. It earns no byte credit until a complete archive and
receiver survive exact parse-back. The first useful measurement is not another lossless coder race;
it is a stratified, serialized quotient candidate with a real distortion price.

Why it is the best remaining rate hypothesis:

- **MEASURED premise:** 63.074% of fx5's archive is the token stream; it is the only section large
  enough to supply 42.4 KB without deleting almost the entire remainder.
- **MEASURED premise:** fixed-field coder work has collapsed to 70 B plus an 18 B transfer.
- **MEASURED negative:** fresh narrow births lose +1.1093 to +1.4806 S on their tested n120
  formulation, so a new small model cannot simply be born from scratch.
- **MEASURED/DERIVED premise:** joint carrier re-solves can change a splice verdict by orders of
  magnitude, so task loss must be inside the representation solve.
- **INFERRED mechanism:** a quotient field can trade exact-token likelihood for task equivalence;
  every current-body coder is forbidden from making that trade.

## PATTERNS OF PATTERNS

### M2 fires a third time

The two gs2 examples were not accidents. JO1 repeated the same error class:

- The design correctly identified a missing logical input.
- The input already existed under a different producer/consumer name and was registered one day
  earlier.
- The wrapper then coupled a cheap producer step to a later implementation and storage blocker.

That is two plumbing defects stacked: **artifact binding** and **stage dependency**. Neither needs
new scorer physics.

### Why Opus moved the pointer while two Codex arms closed

The relevant difference is not model identity. The Opus arm possessed a complete candidate loop:
real payload, actual coder, receiver, deterministic decode, seal, and MAIN fire order. The Codex
arms correctly found that their proposed mechanisms stopped before payload production. Honest
closure in under an hour was preferable to a fake row, but it also exposes where remaining mass
lives:

1. **Not in more global ranking.** r012's census is already enough to kill current-body coding.
2. **Not in more design-only objectives.** JO1's math is built; its producer/receiver path is not.
3. **In retained candidate manufacture:** scorer payloads, one body-changing trainer, actual coder,
   receiver parse-back, and exact stage receipts.

The campaign should therefore spend its next unit turning one new-body hypothesis into retained
bytes, not generating another ten-rung table.

### Positives deflate, negatives strengthen

- fx5 was a real positive and transferred at only 70 B, consuming 79.5% of the 88 B current-body
  ceiling. This is another first-order deflation.
- Fresh width, carrier rank, q3/q4, token drop, deblur, and post-hoc gates now form a stronger
  negative boundary: the next candidate must be warm-started, joint, and body-changing.
